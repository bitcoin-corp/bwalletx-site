Kintsugi

One break. Mended in gold.

A plate can only break once, and no two plates break the same way. Your gold seams are drawn from a hash of your key: one break per account, never repeated, mended in gold.

Your wallet card

Carry your bAvatar on your wallet card.

In bWalletX your bAvatar becomes the background of the card on your home screen, with your $name and your fingerprint on top. Each account gets a numbered card, bWalletX #N · $you, in the order accounts joined.

Status

What works today, and what is next.

Gold bAvatarsLive

Every name gets its art, drawn from its identity key. Same picture in the wallet, on bChatX and in paymail apps.

Art QR on your cardLive

The back of your wallet card is your bAvatar seen through a real QR grid. Tested: 48 of 48 reads across 12 names and 4 sizes.

Flip bAvatarLive

Tap to flip. The back is a clean QR with a bitcoin: pay link or your paymail. Public data only; the app refuses anything key-like.

MintLive

A 1-sat ordinal in your own wallet with your $name and number. Fees paid for the Founding 1,000.

CustomiseLive

Pick styles above, tint it to your theme, or upload your own photo or animated picture.

Animated QR codesComing

Animated QR codes using BRC-141 (fountain-coded air-gap transport, by Darren Kellenschwiler) for passing more than fits in one QR: bAvatar cards, tap-to-pay, sweeps.
