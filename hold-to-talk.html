Hold to talk to b.

The big b in the middle of the bottom bar now listens. Touch and hold it, say what you want, and let go. Your words appear as you speak, and nothing is sent until you lift your finger.

How it works

1 · HOLD

Touch and hold the b

After half a second you feel a tick and a panel opens with gold sound bars. A quick tap still does what it always did: Home in the wallet, or opens b.

2 · SPEAK

Say it

Talk normally. Your words appear in the panel as you speak, so you can see what b will get.

3 · LET GO

Lift your finger

Letting go sends your words to b, just as if you had typed them.

Changed your mind? Slide your finger away from the b and the ring turns grey. Let go there and nothing is sent. Slide back onto the b to keep talking.

The same gesture works on bChatX.com too, in Ask b.

Same rules as typing

  • Nothing is sent until you let go. Slide away and let go, and it is thrown away.
  • Consent first. Before your first message to each AI provider, you are asked to agree, exactly as when you type.
  • Secrets are caught. If you say something that looks like a recovery phrase or private key, it is caught the same way typed secrets are.
  • Prices still show. Paid messages still show the price and your daily limit before anything is spent.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Talking costs nothing extra. What you send to b is priced the same as a typed message, and paid messages show the price and your daily limit first.

What gets sent?

The text of what you said, once you let go. The words are turned into text by your phone or browser’s own speech recognition, then that text goes to b like a typed message.

Which languages?

It listens in your device’s language, using whatever your phone or browser’s speech recognition supports.

When can I use it?

Now. It shipped in bWalletX 5.1.89 on iPhone, Android, the web wallet and the Chrome extension. See the release notes.
