On b · Using Hodlocker with bWalletX

Hodlocker on b

Hodlocker made locking popular: you back a post by time-locking your own BSV against it. The site has gone, but the idea lives on in bWalletX, written in the same on-chain format.

What you can do

Lock behind any post

In the Feed, tap Lock on a post. Pick an amount and a time: a day, a week, a month, or your own (up to about ten years). One approval and it’s done.

Your BSV stays yours

The coins never leave your wallet. They sit in a lock only your key can open, show on the Wallet’s Locks row, and unlock on the date shown.

Same format Hodlocker used

Each lock is the same lockup contract plus a like for the post, the shape Hodlocker wrote and read. Locks on a post are counted from the chain.

Most locked

A board of the most locked people and posts over a day, a week, a month or all time. It counts the posts bWalletX has loaded, and says so.

Status
