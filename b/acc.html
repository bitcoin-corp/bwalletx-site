THE BITCOIN ACCELERATIONIST MOVEMENT

bAcc

Make Bitcoin faster

bAcc is the Bitcoin accelerationist movement. We want Bitcoin to be fast enough and cheap enough to pay for anything, anywhere, all day. Our goal is 1 billion transactions per second (1b TPS). That is a goal, not what the network does today. The long-run version has a name: hyperbitcoinization, Bitcoin becoming the money people use every day.

BTC is slow. BSV is built to be fast.

Both are called Bitcoin. They run the same idea differently, and speed is where they differ most.

BTCBSV
Speed on the main chainAbout 3 to 7 transactions a secondNo fixed limit in the protocol; capacity grows with the hardware
Block sizeCapped by rule at a few megabytesBlocks can be as large as miners and nodes can handle
Fee for a small paymentRises when the network is busy, often too high for small paymentsA fraction of a penny
How it growsMoves small payments to other layersKeeps payments on the main chain and scales it up

Figures are approximate and change over time. Check a current source before you rely on them.

THE GOAL

1b TPS: one billion transactions per second

That is the target bAcc is working toward: a network that never makes anyone wait or pay much to send a payment. Today’s real-world volume is far lower, and we will not pretend otherwise. The speed has to be built, tested and shown.

What bAcc means

More payments

Every payment you send adds to Bitcoin’s volume. Tip, pay, split a bill, buy a track: the movement counts transactions, not speeches.

Smaller payments

Here, Bitcoin payments cost a fraction of a penny, so a one-cent payment makes sense. Small and frequent is how volume grows.

More people

A wallet that is easy to open and quick to use brings in people who have never held Bitcoin. That is why bWalletX exists.

How to take part

1. Get a wallet

bWalletX runs on your phone, in Chrome and on the web. You hold your own keys.

2. Pay in small amounts, often

Send a payment to a friend, tip someone whose work you like, pay for something in Bitcoin. Each one is a transaction.

3. Bring someone with you

The fastest way to raise volume is more people sending. Show one person how to open a wallet.

The Gas Run

The bAcc game mode we are building. Your bike runs on real Bitcoin transactions, and the aim is to keep them flowing without a break.

COMING

1. Fill the tank

Put a small amount of Bitcoin in the gas tank from your wallet, about one US dollar at most per run. What you do not use comes back to your wallet at the end.

COMING

2. Ride

Your bike burns gas by sending real, paid transactions: faster on boost, none when you stop. A crash, or an empty tank, ends the run.

COMING

3. Your score

The longest unbroken chain of your transactions that all get confirmed in a round. It is counted from the blockchain itself, so it cannot be faked.

COMING

4. Rounds

A round follows the blocks, about ten minutes. You see a live count as you ride, marked pending, and the final score when the round closes. Beat the last round’s best.

This mode is not built yet. There are no prizes: the first version is records and bragging rights. You pay normal network fees, nothing more, and every run is capped.
