Culture, made on BSV.

A token isn’t just a ticker. Anything people make can gather around it: a game, pictures, films, songs, a room to talk in and a stage to go live on. In bWalletX all of that sits in one wallet, on a chain that keeps the fees tiny and the content itself on chain.

One token, many kinds of media

Take $FROGGER Example. It’s a made-up coin for this page, not a real token. Here’s what its people could do with it today:

  • A gameGames run inside bWalletX as bApps, so a Frogger game can sit right next to its token. bApps
  • PicturesMint frog art from your phone with bPics, so it’s yours on chain, and share it or keep it. Mint
  • FilmsPost a Frogger film to the bMovies feed; each film can have its own token that backers hold. bMovies
  • MusicAn album on bMusic, where listeners pay the artist directly, a sat a second. Artists
  • A roomHold $FROGGER and its chat room appears in your list. Sell it and the room goes. Token rooms
  • A stageGo live in a Space for holders: speakers, raise-hand, and anyone can listen. bSpaces

Each piece gives people another reason to find the token, hold it and back the people making things for it.

Pay the people who make it

Wherever there’s someone to thank there’s a Tip button: artists, NFT creators, rooms, people in chat and speakers in Spaces. All of it goes to them. On bMusic, listening is paying the artist. Tips

How a culture grows

  1. Launch a coin
  2. Open its room
  3. Make things
  4. Go live
  5. Tip the makers

Launch a coin from the Launchpad, give it something people can use, and its room is there for holders. The wallet won’t sign a token description that promises returns: describe the room and the things you make instead.

Why BSV

  • Tiny fees. A photo costs a fraction of a cent to put on chain; a song can be paid for a sat a second.
  • Scale. Bitcoin SV is built for large blocks, so lots of small payments and posts fit.
  • The content itself is on chain. Pictures, music and video can be stored on chain, not just a link to somewhere else.

Your keys, your culture

bWalletX is non-custodial: the tokens, the art and the tips are held by your own keys. Spaces, bMovies and the Launchpad are not in bWallet, the App Store and Google Play edition.
