Link your X account

Link your X account to your bWalletX to show it's really you. Your wallet gets an X badge and can use your X picture. You make the wallet first, with a passkey or a password; linking X comes after, and it's optional.

@yourhandle→ X badge + photo→ yourname@bwalletx.com

How to do it

  1. Get bWalletX on your phone or computer and create your wallet.
  2. On your wallet's home screen, tap the Link your accounts card. You can also go to Settings › Identity › Link your accounts at any time.
  3. Choose X, sign in on X's own page and allow access.
  4. Back in bWalletX, your X account shows as linked.

The same screen links Google, GitHub and LinkedIn. Linking never changes your handle or your money.

Your name

Linking X doesn't make a name for you. Your handle is a plain yourname@bwalletx.com that you choose, the same as everyone else's, in Settings › Identity › Change handle. X allows underscores in usernames and paymail names don't, so @Jane_Doe would be jane-doe.

Older yourhandle.x@bwalletx.com names, made before October 2026, keep receiving. New ones aren't given out.

What you get

An X badge

Your listing shows that the X account is linked, so people can tell your wallet belongs to the person they follow.

Your picture

Your X profile picture becomes your wallet's picture, so friends recognise you in chats, calls and payments.

Your own token and room

Turn your handle into $YOURNAME, a BSV-21 token with your picture as its icon. Hold the token, join your room.

What X shares

bWalletX reads three things from X: your username, your display name and your profile picture. It never sees your X password, can't post for you and doesn't read your messages. Your wallet's keys are made on your device and never leave it; X has nothing to do with them.

Your 12-word recovery phrase is still the only way to get your money back, so keep it safe.
