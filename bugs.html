What’s broken, what we’re fixing, and what we fixed. Spotted something? Tell us below.

Known issues

  • The b button misbehaves when b disappears

    Investigating

    We are working out where and when it happens. If you see it, tell us what you tapped and on which device.

    Reported 22 Oct 2026

  • Show my 12 words asked for a password you never set

    Fixed in 5.2.40

    If you made your wallet with a passkey, Show my 12 words now has Use my passkey, so you can see your words without a password.

    Fixed 23 Oct 2026

  • Passkey sign-in in the Chrome extension

    Fixed in 5.2.39

    Passkey sign-in was briefly taken out of the extension, then restored in the next release. A user confirmed it works.

    Fixed 22 Oct 2026

  • Crash when opening the wallet after signing out and back in

    Fixed in 5.2.39

    The wallet showed an error about “identityAddress” instead of opening. It now opens normally.

    Fixed 22 Oct 2026

  • Balance stuck on “Still syncing” after a backup at sign-up

    Fixed in 5.2.39

    Making a backup during sign-up no longer stalls your balance, and encrypted backups save every time.

    Fixed 22 Oct 2026

  • Restoring a wallet could hang on Restoring…

    Fixed in 5.2.38

    Restoring or importing a wallet now finishes instead of spinning for good.

    Fixed 20 Oct 2026

  • Sends failed after spending from another device

    Fixed in 5.2.36

    Coins already spent elsewhere could inflate your balance and make sends fail on iPhone. The wallet now clears them when you unlock, and if a send still hits one, it says “tap Send again”.

    Fixed 19 Oct 2026

  • The bChatX Feed crashed on Desktop

    Fixed in 5.2.36

    The Feed opens normally on Desktop.

    Fixed 19 Oct 2026

  • Web wallet layout and sign-in messages

    Fixed in 5.2.34

    Every screen stays in its column, a wrong password says so, and after sign-up you go straight to your wallet.

    Fixed 19 Oct 2026

  • bMail could lose or double-pay a stamp

    Fixed in 5.2.33

    A retry now reuses the same stamp, so a stamp is never lost or paid twice.

    Fixed 18 Oct 2026

  • Token sends failed with “OP_DUP”

    Fixed in 5.2.32

    The wallet repairs the token record and tries again once.

    Fixed 18 Oct 2026

  • “Load failed” on the iPhone wallet screen

    Fixed in 5.2.30

    Busy lookups are retried, and any failure now names the service it couldn’t reach instead of a bare “Load failed”.

    Fixed 17 Oct 2026

  • Black screen after unlock

    Fixed in 5.2.25

    If something goes wrong after unlock you now see a safety screen, not a blank page. The BSV price also no longer fails under load.

    Fixed 16 Oct 2026

  • Phone apps refused to open their own pages

    Fixed in 5.2.20

    Pages no longer stop with a “Reserved originator” message.

    Fixed 14 Oct 2026

  • Payments blocked by an old transaction

    Fixed in 5.2.19

    A stale transaction no longer blocks new payments.

    Fixed 14 Oct 2026

  • Minting a name could wait forever

    Fixed in 5.2.17

    If the network never received a mint, the wallet now lets it go and tells you.

    Fixed 13 Oct 2026

  • Big NFT mints failed

    Fixed in 5.2.15

    They now show Pending while the network catches up, and a stuck send releases its coins.

    Fixed 12 Oct 2026

  • Chat list jumped while loading

    Fixed in 5.2.11

    Chats open at once and the list stays still.

    Fixed 12 Oct 2026

  • Fingerprint unlock stopped working on Android

    Fixed in 5.2.10

    It works again, and asks you to set it up again when needed.

    Fixed 12 Oct 2026
