Pay as you listen. Stop any time.

Some things are worth a little, for as long as they last. In bWalletX you pay by the second or the minute for a Space, a call or a song. You set a limit once. The money goes straight to the people you hear.

How it works

See the price

The rate is shown before you start: per second, per minute or per hour.

Set your limit

Approve once, with the most you’ll spend. No popup for each payment.

Stop when you like

Leave, hang up or tap Stop. Payments end there.

Paid Spaces

A host can ask listeners to pay for a live Space. They set the price per second, minute or hour, in BSV (priced in dollars) or in a token.

  • The money is split between the people on stage, or paid to the token’s issuer, as the host chooses.
  • The first minute is usually free, so you can hear it first.
  • You choose “Most I’ll spend this session”. Or take just the free minute.
  • Your wallet pays each minute directly to the speakers. bChatX takes nothing.
  • It stops at your limit, or when you leave.

See bSpaces.

Paid calls

With bPhone, people can charge to take a call: a coach, a tutor, an adviser. They set a rate per second, minute, hour or per call, in BSV, MNEE or a token.

  • You see the rate and your most you’ll spend before the call rings.
  • You pay as you talk, wallet to wallet. In BSV, a small payment goes every 10 seconds.
  • Hang up and the payments stop.

See bPhone.

Music by the second

On VexVoid’s album Echoes in the Abyss, the headline track is free. Pay-to-play tracks cost 1 sat a second to the artist, plus the network fee.

  • One approval sets a budget. After that, every second is its own small payment, with no popups.
  • You see what’s gone to the artist, the network fees and the budget left.
  • Tap Stop, leave, or hide the page for a minute, and what’s left comes back to your wallet.

See Artists.

Your money, your limit

You never pay more than the limit you set.

Each payment is made out to the speakers, the person you’re calling, or the artist.
