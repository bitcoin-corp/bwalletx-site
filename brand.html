Using our brand

Please do

  • Use the logo to link to bWalletX, or to show that your app works with it.
  • Use the sign-in buttons for “Sign in with bWalletX”.
  • Give the logo some space, and keep it at least 16 px tall.
  • Write the name as bWalletX: small b, capital W, capital X.

Please don’t

  • Redraw, stretch, rotate or recolour the b.
  • Add an X, outlines or effects to the mark.
  • Suggest that we endorse or run your product unless we do.
  • Use our name or logo in your own product’s name.
