02 · LOGOThe gold b.

Our logo is the letter b alone, drawn from two simple shapes: a stem with a 45° flag cut across the top, and a round bowl. There is no X in the mark; the X lives in the name.

  1. The stem is 18 units wide, cut at 45° across the top: the bCorp flag.
  2. The bowl is a circle (radius 33) with a round counter (radius 15).
  3. They meet cleanly. The stem ends exactly where the bowl’s edge is upright, so the left side runs in one unbroken line.
  4. The gold runs top to bottom across the whole letter: #FFE58A, #FFD24D, #C98F00.
xx

Clear space: keep at least x clear all round, where x is the width of the counter (the hole). Minimum size: 16 px tall on screen, 6 mm in print.

ABOUT bWALLETX

bWalletX is a Bitcoin wallet for everyday life. People hold their own keys, pay and get paid in seconds, set money aside in pots, chat, and sign in to websites with their wallet. It runs on the web, as a Chrome extension and on Android. bWalletX is made in London by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd.
