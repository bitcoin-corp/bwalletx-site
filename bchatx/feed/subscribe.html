bChatX · Feed subscriptions

Your news, first. Paid for, by the people who want it.

The bChatX Open Feed already carries posts from CoinGeek, Twetch, Treechat, Peck, Hodlocker, RelayX and more. Feed subscriptions let you sell yours. Subscribers see your posts before anyone else, or see posts that nobody else gets, and they pay you straight to your wallet.

Two things you can sell

⏱Early accessSubscribers see a post minutes or hours before it reaches the public feed. You pick the head start. For news, being first is the whole point.
🔒Subscriber-only postsSome posts never go public: analysis, scoops, behind-the-scenes, the full story. Only subscribers can open them.

Why sell your feed here

You keep the readerYour posts carry your name and logo and link back to you. bChatX is a window onto your service, not a copy of it.
A cent a dayThe normal price is $0.01 a day per subscription, so following a favourite source is easy to say yes to. 100,000 subscribers comes to $1,000 a day.
Paid to your walletSubscribers pay in BSV from bWalletX, straight to your address. There are no card fees and no 30-day payout wait.
Readers who already payEvery bChatX reader has a wallet. Subscribing takes one tap. They don't need a card, an email or a new account.
A receipt on chainEvery subscription is a payment anyone can check. You can see exactly who paid and when.
