Token locking. Lock tokens so nobody can dump early.

Lock BSV is live: BSV locked until a block height, and nobody can move it before then, not even you. Token locking does the same for tokens.

Live Personal token locks shipped in 5.2.46. Creator vesting at launch shipped in 5.2.32. Locked airdrops are still coming.

Creator vesting at launch: prove you won’t dump

When you launch a coin, you can keep a share for yourself, locked so you can’t sell it straight away. Live since 5.2.32, in bWalletX only (the Launchpad):

  • LiveCreator allocation: 0 to 20% of the supply, set by the creator. The default is 10%.
  • LiveAlways time-locked. The default is a 12-month cliff (nothing unlocks for a year), then monthly unlocks over 24 months. The shortest lock allowed is 6 months.
  • LiveA public receipt. The coin’s page shows how much the creator locked and until when, linked to the locks on chain, so anyone can check them.

Personal token locks

Live Lock BSV-21 tokens until a date, with an on-chain receipt. In the Wallet, swipe a token row and tap Lock. Your token locks are listed in Locks & Pots › Locks, under Token locks. On a new device, Find my token locks finds them again from your recovery phrase. Live since 5.2.46.

Locked airdrops

Coming Airdrops that unlock over time: each recipient gets their own lock that they can claim once it opens. See Airdrops.

What it isn’t

A lock only stops tokens moving before a date. It says nothing about what a token is worth, now or later.
