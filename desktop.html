Built for a keyboard

  • ⌘KAsk b. Open the b agent from anywhere and tell it what to do.
  • ⌘LLock the wallet. One shortcut when you step away.
  • AgentsAdd agent account. Give an AI agent its own account in your wallet, from the sidebar.
  • DevConnect CLI & MCP. Link the bwalletx CLI and MCP tools from the sidebar.

Install it as an app

Installed, it gets its own window and dock or taskbar icon. Nothing to download from a store.

  1. Chrome or Edge: open desktop.bwalletx.com and click Install app at the top, or the install icon in the address bar.
  2. Safari on Mac: choose File › Add to Dock.

Your keys stay in your browser

Your keys are kept encrypted in the browser on your computer. desktop.bwalletx.com is a separate site from web.bwalletx.com, so a wallet you already have does not appear by itself: restore it with its recovery phrase. Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline.
