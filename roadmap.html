Roadmap.

What bWalletX does today, what we are building now, and what is planned. No dates: things move to Live when they ship.

Live Live now

Shipped and working today. Every release is listed on Releases.

Wallet

  • Web wallet, Android app, Chrome extension and a Mac Desktop app
  • Sign in with a passkey (Face ID or fingerprint)
  • Send to a $name or address, with dollars shown first on every approval
  • Encrypted backups and restore from your 12 words
  • Lock BSV and tokens until a date, recoverable from your 12 words
  • Pots, standing orders and subscriptions, with one switch to pause spending
  • bTrust: recovery slices for people you trust and an inheritance plan
  • Themes and a card designer, with theme packs in the Store
  • Choose what sits on your home screen
  • Restore old RelayX wallets and find their NFTs
  • NFTs: view, send, receive, full-screen viewer and 3D models
  • Swap into BSV and Buy BSV from the wallet
  • Exchange: markets, token pages with charts, swipe to buy, sell or chat
  • Launch your own coin with a holders’ room and a launch curve
  • Airdrop tokens or BSV to every holder with one approval
  • The Store: shops, departments and themes paid in BSV
  • Sign in with bWalletX for other sites

Chat

  • Token rooms with live chat, in the wallet and on bChatX
  • Live Spaces with camera and screen sharing, and a calendar of scheduled Spaces
  • Calls with screen sharing
  • bMail: one inbox with price-to-reach and signed agreements
  • Feed: posts from bChatX, CoinGeek, Hodlocker and RelayX, with tips
  • Tip buttons on posts, tracks and rooms
  • Address book with import and export, and finding friends who already use bWalletX

Music

  • bMusicX: album store, artist pages and free first tracks
  • Pay-per-second listening, and Back this artist pays the artist directly
  • Artist rank by money paid to the artist
  • Add a release: publish your music with a square cover, on chain

Video

  • bVideoX: creators record their videos on chain in their own wallet
  • Full-screen video reels in the Feed
  • Video NFTs with cover art that play on iPhone

Games

  • bGamesX: games and game tokens in one place
  • TERRORNODE: play to earn its coin, with its own room and shop
  • TokenBlaster: launch and trade coins, with a TokenBlaster shop in the Store
  • Game rooms dressed in each game’s colours

Names

  • Get a $name that is also your paymail and chat handle
  • Names are signed by your own wallet, with a public log of every change
  • Name Store: buy, sell and give names
  • Link your X and Google accounts to your name

Agents and b

  • Ask b: talk or type to b in the wallet
  • Report to b: bug reports that never get lost
  • Agent accounts for your own bots, with the CLI and MCP server
  • bBots
  • Strategies with wallet-enforced rules and paper mode
  • Business agent from a single link, with checkout in chat

Site

  • Release notes for every version
  • b Creative: campaign films and pages
  • bApps directory
  • Ideas and bug boards
  • Developer guides and open-source kit
  • Brand guide
