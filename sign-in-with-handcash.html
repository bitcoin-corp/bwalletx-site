Sign in with HandCash. Bring your name with you.

Already have a HandCash account? Use it to sign in to bChatX. You keep your name and your friends, and you get your own bWalletX address.

Open bChatXGet bWalletX

How it works

  • Tap More waysOn bchatx.com, under the gold bWalletX button.
  • Approve in HandCashHandCash confirms it’s really you. We use your verified account, never a name you type.
  • You’re inYour account is linked to that HandCash id, and you get first claim on the same name at bwalletx.com.

What you get

  • First claim on your name. If you’re $alice on HandCash, alice@bwalletx.com is held for you first.
  • A new bWalletX address. It’s your own wallet, not a forward to HandCash. Payments to name@bwalletx.com land in bWalletX.
  • Your HandCash friends. Bring them into your bWalletX address book, so the people you already know are there from day one.

Keep it safe

  • Your HandCash login stays on your phone. The token is kept in the phone’s secure storage.
  • It expires. When it does, you simply sign in with HandCash again.
  • We never see your HandCash password. HandCash checks who you are; we only get the verified id.
