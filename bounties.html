How the pool works

  • There is a fixed pool each calendar quarter. The bar shows what is left, read live from the pool address on chain.
  • The first valid report of an issue earns the reward. Duplicates earn credit.
  • Rewards are paid after the fix ships.
  • When a quarter's pool is spent, valid reports are paid from the next quarter's pool, in the order they were triaged.

The full policy, the reward table and the safe harbour are on b-Aware.
