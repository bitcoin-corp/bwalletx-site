RelayX on b

RelayX has closed, but what you held there is still on chain, under the keys in your RelayX 12 words. bWalletX reads the chain for those keys and gives it back to you.

What you can do

Find your NFTs

Wallet › Bring in old assets › Recover RelayX collections. Enter your 12 words and tap Find my NFTs. Your NFTs appear as a gallery, grouped by collection. Finding is read-only: nothing moves.

Keep them in bWalletX

Add account › Restore › RelayX. A Your RelayX collection card appears at the top of that account; one tap finds your NFTs again, and they sit in the NFTs tab under Run.

Move leftover BSV

If your old RelayX addresses still hold BSV, one tap moves it into your account. RelayX NFTs are recognised first and never spent as coins.

Cancel old listings

NFTs still listed for sale on RelayX when it closed are found too. The cancel is built and signed on your phone and shown before you allow it; one tap brings the NFT back to your own address.

Read the old RelayX club posts

Feed › RelayX. The posts people wrote in RelayX clubs are on chain, so you can read them again, reply, like and lock behind them. RelayX itself is gone, so there is no “open original” link.
