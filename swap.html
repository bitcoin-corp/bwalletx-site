Swap into BSV.

In bWalletX you can swap BTC, ETH, USDT, SOL and 1,000+ other coins into BSV. The BSV is paid straight into your own wallet. Nobody holds your coins along the way: you send a deposit, BSV arrives at your address.

How it works

Pick a coin and amount

Choose the coin you're swapping from and how much. The app shows an estimate of the BSV you'll get, fees included.

Send the deposit

bWalletX gives you a one-off deposit address. Send the coin to it from any wallet or exchange.

BSV lands in your wallet

Usually within 10 to 60 minutes. Track each swap in the app until it's done.

Who runs the swap

Swaps are run by ChangeNOW, not bWalletX. ChangeNOW may pause a swap for AML/KYC checks and ask for details before releasing or refunding it.

Rates float: the amount shown is an estimate until your deposit arrives. We make no promises about the price of any coin.
