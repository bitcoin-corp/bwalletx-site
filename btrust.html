A Lock with more than one key

A Lock keeps money aside until a date, and only you hold the key. A bTrust shares the keys. You choose who holds one, say your solicitor and two family members, and how many it takes to open it: any two of three, for example.

Nobody can move it alone. Not one of them, and not even you.

Looked after in life

Lose your phone, and two of your key holders can help you back in. No company to call, no forms, no waiting.

Looked after later

Choose who receives what, and when: on a date, or if you haven’t checked in for a while. Leave each of them a letter in your own words. When the moment comes, your key holders open it together and carry out your wishes.

Your important papers

Keep the things your family will need alongside it: deeds, insurance, the letter you’d want found, and the backup words for wallets you keep elsewhere. Everything is encrypted on your device, and only opens for the people you’ve chosen, when the time comes.
