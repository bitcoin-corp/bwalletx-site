What a bWalletX store is

Think of the Store as a small shopping centre inside the wallet. Every shop has its own front. Every item also sits in a department, so people can browse by shop (“Ninja Punk Girls”) or by kind of thing (“Themes”).

It’s where your buyers already are.

No new app to download and no account to make. People who use bWalletX open Exchange › Store and your shop is there, next to the others.

Paying takes one tap.

A buyer sees one sheet with the price and taps Allow. No card numbers and no checkout form. The money is BSV, straight from their wallet.

What they buy stays with them.

A theme stays theirs to switch on whenever they like. An NFT sits in their wallet’s NFTs tab. Each sale also comes with a signed receipt.

  1. Banner. The wide picture across the top, 1500 × 500. Your art, your scene, your mood.
  2. Logo. A square, 512 × 512. It also shows on your shop’s tile in the Store and on your buyers’ receipts.
  3. Shop name and a line about you. Up to 40 characters for the name, a sentence or two for the rest.

  5. Items. Each one has a picture, a title, a short description and a price in dollars. Buyers pay the same value in BSV.

Your shop also gets its own web address, bwalletx.com/shop/your-name. You pick the name when you start.

Who it’s for

  • NFT collections. You already have the art and the fans. Give them somewhere to buy, and something to do with what they own.
  • Artists and illustrators. Sell your pictures, and themes made from them, so fans carry your work on their phone.
  • Musicians and film makers. Tracks, albums, clips, and a look for the wallet to match.
  • Game makers. Sell the cars, skins and extras for your game where your players keep their money.
  • Brands and communities. One place for your members to pick up the official look and the good stuff.

  • You set prices in dollars. The buyer pays the same value in BSV at the rate on the day.
  • Your share goes to the wallet your shop belongs to: the one you signed in with. We don’t hold it for you.

  • Starting a shop is free, with no monthly fee.

Payouts to sellers start when the first outside shops open. Today the Store sells only our own shops’ themes.

Questions

What does it cost to start?
Nothing. When you sell something, 80% goes to you and 20% to the Store.
Do I need a bWalletX?
Yes. Your shop belongs to your wallet, and that’s where your money arrives. Get bWalletX for the web, Chrome, Android or iPhone. It’s free.
Who can see my draft?
Only you, signed in with the same wallet. Nobody else sees it until your shop opens.
Why does b review shops?
So buyers can trust what they find: real makers, no fakes, nothing pretending to be someone it isn’t.
Is a theme mine for good?
Yes. Pay once and it’s yours to keep. Switch between it and the free themes whenever you like.
Why can’t I see the Store in bWallet?
The Store is in bWalletX: web, Chrome extension, Android and iPhone. It isn’t in the App Store and Google Play edition, bWallet.
