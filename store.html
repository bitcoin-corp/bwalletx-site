How it works

The Store is a mall. Each shop has its own banner, logo and items. Each item also sits in a department, so you can browse by shop or by kind of thing. Pick an item, approve one payment sheet, and it’s yours. A theme switches on straight away and stays yours to keep. The first sale went through on 17 October 2026.

The Store is in bWalletX (web, Chrome extension, Android and iPhone). It is not in the App Store and Google Play edition, bWallet.

Sell in the Store

Coming · shops open soon

You will be able to open your own shop. Here is what you’ll need, so you can get ready now.

What you can sell

  • Anything that fits a department: themes, subscriptions, stickers and emoji, art and editions, music and video, courses and books, rooms and memberships, services.

What a good shop needs

  • A shop name.
  • A logo: 512 × 512 PNG.
  • A banner: 1500 × 500.
  • A short blurb about you or your shop.
  • At least 3 products, each with a title, a 1–2 line description, a price in $ and a preview image.
  • For a theme: its colours (accent, background, surface, text) and the card art.

Rules

  • Digital goods only.
  • Your own work, or work you have the rights to sell.
  • No pretending to be a brand or person you are not.
  • b reviews every shop and every listing before it goes live.

Money

  • Buyers pay in BSV from their wallet.
  • 80% to the seller, 20% to the Store.
  • Your share is paid to your bWalletX.

Questions

Is a theme mine for good?
Yes. Pay once and it’s yours to keep. Switch between it and the free themes whenever you like.
How do I pay?
In BSV, from your bWalletX balance. You see one sheet with the price, tap Allow, and the theme switches on.
Why can’t I see the Store in bWallet?
The Store is in bWalletX only: web, Chrome extension, Android and iPhone. It isn’t in the App Store and Google Play edition.
When can I sell?
Shops open soon. Get your logo, banner and three products ready using the list above.
