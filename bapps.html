bApps: apps that run inside bWalletX

bApps are Bitcoin apps from The Bitcoin Corporation. Open them from the Apps tab in bWalletX, or in any browser. You sign in with your wallet, not a password, and anything you pay comes from your wallet, after you approve it.

How bApps work

Sign in with your wallet Live

One tap on "Sign in with bWalletX". No account, no password, no email.

Pay from your wallet Live

When an app asks for money, bWalletX shows you what it wants. Nothing leaves without your approval.

One bar in every app Coming

Every bApp gets the same five buttons in the same place, each one about the app you have open:

WalletExchangebFeedChat

An app that doesn't use a button shows it greyed out, so the bar always looks the same. ☰ (your wallet menu) stays top left, with the app's icon next to it.

Like = 1¢ to join the room Coming

Each app's Feed shows what's trending. A Like is a small buy, 1¢ by default (or one PNEE), of that thing's token. It funds the thing you liked, and the token is your key to its chat room. Daily cap of $1 by default; you can change both.
