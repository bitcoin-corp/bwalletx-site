02 — Design your card

Your card, your art.

The card at the top of your wallet can carry any art you like. The white set keeps it minimal: a white card, black type, and art washed back so your balance stays easy to read. The wave is Hokusai’s Great Wave off Kanagawa (1831), which is in the public domain.

03 — The mark

One clean outline b.

In the white theme the b is drawn as a single outline: the stem and the bowl are joined into one shape, and the hole in the middle is its own ring. No lines cross inside it. It sits on a white app tile to match.
