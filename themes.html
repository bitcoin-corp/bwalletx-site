03 / 08  Branded editions

An edition of your own.

Three ways to theme a wallet. Free: colours, ready-made themes and emoji cards for your own wallet. Premium themes ($1 each in the Store; NPG, NPGX and VexVoid today): animated, limited-edition and creator themes you own; the money goes to the creator, whether an artist or a token issuer. Editions ($99 a year, made by us): for businesses and token communities, a shareable branded wallet for every holder with its own address (wallet.yourcoin.com), name, card art, icon and your token shown first, featured in Editions and your token’s room. Hold a token and its community theme unlocks free. Every user still gets bWallet free. Editions are ordered by message today; ordering inside the wallet is coming.

05 / 08  Design your card

Your card, your art.

The card at the top of your wallet can carry any art you like. The white set keeps it minimal: a white card, black type, and art washed back so your balance stays easy to read. The wave is Hokusai’s Great Wave off Kanagawa (1831), which is in the public domain.

06 / 08  The mark

One clean outline b.

In the white theme the b is drawn as a single outline: the stem and the bowl are joined into one shape, and the hole in the middle is its own ring. No lines cross inside it. It sits on a white app tile to match.
