Tip the people who make things.

bWalletX has a Tip button wherever there’s someone to thank: artists, NFT creators, rooms, people in chat, friends and speakers in Spaces. All of it goes to them.

How it works

Tap Tip

Next to an artist, a track, a creator, a room, a name in chat, a friend or a speaker.

Pick an amount

10p, 50p, £1 or £5 (or 10¢, 50¢, $1, $5), or your own. Add a note if you like.

Allow once

One sheet, one Allow. bWalletX works out the BSV at today’s price and sends it.

Where you’ll find it

  • bMusic: the artist page, each track and the album player. See Artists.
  • NFTs: the creator, when the NFT names one. See NFTs.
  • Chat: the room’s creator, and each person by their name.
  • Friends: on each friend’s page. See Friends.
  • Spaces: each speaker on stage. See bSpaces.

You won’t see a Tip button on your own things.

All of it goes to them

The tip goes from your wallet to theirs. We take nothing.

If the network is slow, the wallet says the tip is on its way, so you don’t pay twice.

Where to find it
