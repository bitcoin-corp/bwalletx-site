How it will work

1. Who you are

Continue with X, Google or HandCash. This only says who you are and links your name and badge. It never touches your keys.

2. What locks it

A passkey made on your device locks the wallet. You unlock with your face, your finger or your PIN.

3. Your 12 words, kept safe

Your wallet still has its 12 words underneath. An encrypted copy is saved that only your passkey can open. You can show the words any time.

Same wallet on phone and computer

Passkeys sync with your Apple or Google account, so the same passkey opens the same wallet on your other devices. On a computer, you can also scan a code with your phone to open it.

The classic way stays

A password and 12 words will still be there, side by side. Pick whichever suits you. Restoring with your 12 words or a backup file works exactly as it does today.

We never hold your keys

Your keys are made on your device. The encrypted copy of your 12 words can only be opened by your passkey, and the passkey never leaves your device or your Apple or Google account. We can’t open your wallet, and we can’t move your money. If we disappeared, your 12 words would still restore your wallet in any compatible app.

Questions

What if I lose my phone?

Your passkey lives in your Apple or Google account, not only on the phone. Sign in on a new phone, use the same passkey, and your wallet comes back. Your 12 words still work too. For larger amounts we’ll suggest you write them down as well.

Can bWalletX open my wallet?

No. Only your passkey can open the encrypted copy of your words, and we never see your keys.

Do my old wallets still work?

Yes, unchanged. Your password, your 12 words and your backups keep working exactly as before.
