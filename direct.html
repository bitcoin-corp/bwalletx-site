BWALLET DIRECT

Direct

Sign in straight from your own wallet

bWallet Direct is a bWalletX wallet you open with a passkey, and the way a website lets you in with it. No account to make, no password to invent, no middleman holding your login. Your wallet proves it is you; the site checks it.

Make a wallet with a passkey Add it to your site

For customers

1. Make your wallet with a passkey

Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN opens it. Nothing to write down first. How passkey sign-in works.

2. Press “Sign in with bWalletX” on a site

Your wallet is found in your browser, in the app, or by scanning a QR code with your phone, and it asks you to approve.

3. You are in

The site gets a signature from your wallet’s identity key, nothing else: no email, name or password.

For businesses

One script tag and two routes on your server. There is no API key, no client ID and no account with us: your server checks the signature itself. The same wallet gives the same ID every time they come back.

Good to know

Passkeys stay in your Apple or Google account

A passkey saved to iCloud cannot open a wallet on an Android phone, and the other way round. Keep your recovery file or 12 words as well.

Not the same as the Android “direct” download

That is the bWalletX Android app you download from this site instead of Google Play. bWallet Direct is the sign-in service.
