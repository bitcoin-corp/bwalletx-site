Transcript

The narration below is the voiceover script the video was recorded from (both voices read the same words). The “on screen” lines are a short text summary of what each scene shows. The video was recorded on the web app with a fresh test wallet, so balances show $0.00. About 73 seconds.

  1. On screen: The gold b logo fades up on black, with the title bWalletX.

    This is bWalletX. One wallet for your money, your tokens, your apps and your AI agent.

  2. On screen: Create wallet: a password is typed and the wallet opens.

    Making a wallet takes a few seconds. Your keys are created on your device, and they never leave it.

  3. On screen: The Back up your wallet screen: Save an encrypted backup, then the share sheet.

    Before you can receive money, it asks you to back up: an encrypted file you can save or email to yourself, or your twelve words on paper.

  4. On screen: Wallet home: the balance in dollars, Receive, Send and Mint, and the token list.

    Balances are in dollars. Send to a name instead of a long address, and keep BSV, MNEE and all your tokens in one place.

  5. On screen: The Exchange tab: Tokens, NFTs, Music and Video, with trending tokens.

    The Exchange lets you buy and sell tokens and NFTs, wallet to wallet, with no middleman.

  6. On screen: Exchange, Music and Video: music and movie NFT listings.

    Music and movie NFTs trade here too, and play in your wallet’s media player once you own them.

  7. On screen: The Apps tab: the grid of bApps.

    The Apps tab is a store of BSV apps, and you can add any site you like.

  8. On screen: The Feed of posts from BSV social apps, then Chat and its group rooms.

    The Feed shows posts from across BSV social apps. In Chat, join group rooms, or hold a friend’s token to get into theirs.

  9. On screen: The b agent page with its quick-start buttons.

    And b, your AI assistant, is built in. Pay a cent a message, or add your own AI key for more privacy.

  10. On screen: A reply from b setting out goals and limits.

    It writes the rules, and the wallet enforces the limits before anything is signed.

  11. On screen: End card: bwalletx.com, Android, iPhone, Chrome and Web, and a beta note.

    bWalletX. Get it free at bwalletx.com. It’s in beta, so start with small amounts.
