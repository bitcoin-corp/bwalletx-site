Security at bWalletX.

Your money should be yours alone. Here is how bWalletX keeps it that way, what is live today, and how to tell us if you find a problem.

Where we stand today

  • LiveKeys made and kept on your device, encrypted with your password.
  • LiveOpen-source wallet code (MIT) that anyone can read.
  • LiveOngoing internal review of the money and key paths.
  • ComingIndependent security audit.

How bWalletX protects you

  • KeysYour keys are created on your device and stay there, encrypted with your password.
  • ServersOur servers never hold keys that can move your money.
  • ApprovalEvery payment and every signature needs your approval, on one sheet that shows what you are agreeing to.
  • RecoveryYour 12 words restore your wallet, in bWalletX or in other compatible wallets, even if we disappeared.
  • OpenThe wallet is open source under the MIT licence, so anyone can check what it does: bitcoin-corp/bwallet and the phone edition b0ase/yours-mobile. More on the open source page.
  • Built onThe open-source Yours Wallet and the BSV Association’s open wallet libraries.

Report a vulnerability

Email support@bwalletx.com with “SECURITY” in the subject.

Please include:

  • what you found and why it matters;
  • the steps to reproduce it, and which edition and version (web, extension, iPhone, Android, desktop);
  • any proof of concept, screenshots or transaction IDs from your own test wallets;
  • how you would like to be credited, if at all.

We will reply within 3 working days, keep you updated while we fix it, and tell you when it is fixed.

Please play fair

  • Don’t disclose the issue publicly for 90 days, or until it is fixed, whichever comes first.
  • Never access, move or change other people’s funds or data. If you reach someone else’s data by accident, stop, and tell us.
  • Test only with your own wallets and small amounts.
  • No social engineering, phishing, physical attacks or denial of service.

Safe harbour

When conducting vulnerability research according to this policy, we consider this research conducted under this policy to be:

  • Authorized in view of any applicable anti-hacking laws, and we will not initiate or support legal action against you for accidental, good faith violations of this policy;
  • Authorized in view of relevant anti-circumvention laws, and we will not bring a claim against you for circumvention of technology controls;
  • Exempt from restrictions in our Terms of Use (https://bwalletx.com/terms) that would interfere with conducting security research, and we waive those restrictions on a limited basis; and
  • Lawful, helpful to the overall security of the Internet, and conducted in good faith.

You are expected, as always, to comply with all applicable laws. If legal action is initiated by a third party against you and you have complied with this policy, we will take steps to make it known that your actions were conducted in compliance with this policy.

If at any time you have concerns or are uncertain whether your security research is consistent with this policy, please submit a report through support@bwalletx.com (subject “SECURITY”) before going any further.
