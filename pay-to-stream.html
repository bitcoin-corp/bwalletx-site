A free preview, always

Every paid podcast starts with something free: the first minute, or a clip the host picks. Hosts choose it with simple tools, like trimming a clip on YouTube: drag a start, drag an end, done.

Two ways to listen

Ticket

One price, shown up front. Pay once and listen in full, as often as you like.

Pay as you listen

A tiny payment every 30 to 60 seconds while it plays. Pause or leave and the payments stop.

Your Listening budget

Paying by the minute uses bWalletX’s streaming payments. You set a Listening budget once, and nothing goes over it.

No popup for every payment. Stop any time and you stop paying.

For hosts

  • Recording is opt-in. A Space only becomes a podcast if the host says so.
  • Pick a ticket price, a per-minute rate, or both.
  • Choose the free preview: the first minute, or your own clip.
  • Payments go to you, wallet to wallet.
