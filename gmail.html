Link your Google account

Link your Google account to your bWalletX to show it's really you. Your wallet gets a Google badge and can use your Google picture. You make the wallet first; linking Google comes after, and it's optional. You choose your own handle, and your email address never becomes part of it.

Google account→ Google badge + photo→ yourname@bwalletx.com

How to do it

  1. Get bWalletX on your phone or computer and create your wallet.
  2. On your wallet's home screen, tap the Link your accounts card, or go to Settings › Identity › Link your accounts.
  3. Choose Google and pick your account on Google's own sign-in page.
  4. Back in bWalletX, your Google account shows as linked.

Linking never changes your handle or your money.

Your name

Signing in with Google doesn't make a name for you. Your handle is a plain yourname@bwalletx.com that you choose. We stopped making names from Gmail addresses because they showed your email address to anyone who saw the name.

Older yourname.gmail@bwalletx.com names, made before October 2026, keep receiving, and the app hides most of the name where others can see it. New ones aren't given out.

What you get

A Google badge

Your listing shows that a Google account is linked, so people know a real account stands behind your wallet.

Your picture

Your Google profile picture becomes your wallet's picture, so friends recognise you in chats, calls and payments.

Your own token and room

Turn your handle into $YOURNAME, a BSV-21 token with your picture as its icon. Hold the token, join your room.

What Google shares

bWalletX asks Google for three things only: your email address, your name and your profile picture. It never sees your Google password and can't read your email, contacts or files. Your wallet's keys are made on your device and never leave it; Google has nothing to do with them.

Your 12-word recovery phrase is still the only way to get your money back, so keep it safe.
