How the numbers are made

  1. Items. Every public page on bwalletx.com that is in the sitemap. Internal company matters are left out on purpose.
  2. Commits. Every commit since the project began on 3 Oct 2026, from: bwalletx.com site (887 commits), bWalletX app (1,672 commits), bChatX (635 commits), bMailX site (13 commits), bMarketX site (34 commits), bGitX site (4 commits). A site commit counts towards the pages and share images it touched. An app commit counts towards a page when a file path names that feature (for example a pots folder counts towards Pots). A commit that touches several items is split evenly between them. App commits that match no page go into "shared code". Commit messages are never published.
  3. Time taken. Commits are grouped into sessions: a gap of more than 2 hours starts a new one. Each session counts as last commit minus first commit, plus 30 minutes for the work before the first commit. Elapsed days and PR open-to-merge times are shown for context only.
  4. Cost. Hours × the Kintsugi rate of $20 an hour, paid in BSV at the BSV/USD rate on the day of payment. BSV figures are approximate, converted at $17.01 per BSV, the price fetched once from WhatsOnChain on 30 Oct 2026. This is what the same output would cost an AI-assisted coder on Kintsugi, not what it cost us.
  5. Limits. Commit gaps miss design and thinking time, and quick AI-assisted bursts look short. Treat each figure as a rough range.
