Kintsugi (金継ぎ): mending broken pottery with gold, so the repair shows.

Kintsugi program

Overview · Work done so far

A program for developers who build with Claude. Verify your ID, show your setup, pass one entry task, and take paid work on bWalletX, bChatX and the bApps.

Apply See paid bounties

Who it is for

Developers who already work AI-first with Claude Code and want steady, small, well-defined jobs paid in Bitcoin. You choose which tasks to take and when to work.

How work arrives

From b

b reads bug reports and gaps across our apps and turns them into tasks: what to change, how to test it, how long it should take.

From bounties

People post bounties for features and fixes they want. Each one carries a tag your pull request quotes to claim it.

The pay

$20 an hour, paid in BSV

Converted at the BSV/USD rate on the day you are paid. Each task is priced before you start, from its estimate, and the price is written into the contract. You are paid to your $handle in bWalletX when the work is accepted.

Four steps

  1. A verified ID. Identity check by Veriff. We keep the result, not your documents.
  2. The right tools. A paid Anthropic Claude plan with Claude Code. No local models, no ChatGPT. Link your GitHub, declare your plan, and your Kintsugi commits carry the Co-Authored-By: Claude line.
  3. The entry test. One real task, done to spec in Claude Code and reviewed by us. Pass it and you are Kintsugi Level 1.
  4. Get certified. Levels go up with accepted work. Level 3 builders review others.

Three signatures on every job

The client

Wants the work and funds the task.

The developer

Builds it to the spec and opens the pull request.

Kintsugi

Checks the spec, holds the payment, reviews the work and releases the pay.

All three sign the same contract in bChatX before work starts. It sets the task, the price, the deadline and what counts as done. Delivered work belongs to the client once it is paid for.

Where it stands

  • Bounties paid in public, every payout linked to its transaction (/bounties)
  • Veriff identity checks
  • GitHub account linking
  • Contracts signed by several parties in bChatX
  • Pull requests claimed against a bounty tag
  • Developer applications and the entry test
  • Hourly pricing and held payment for Kintsugi tasks
  • Developer and client terms
