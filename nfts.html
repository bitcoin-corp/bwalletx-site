Everything you own, edge to edge.

bWalletX Desktop keeps every NFT you own in one library. Pictures and clips open in their own shape and fill the space. No frames: the screen is the frame.

One library

See it all

Pictures, video, music and documents in one grid, each with a preview.

Find it fast

Filter by kind, open a collection, or search. NFTs still on their way are marked.

Open it

The picture or clip fills the space. Details and actions sit around it, not on top.

Tickets and credits have their own tabs next to it, so the grid is just your things.

Plays straight away

Video plays where it is. If you’ve just been sent something and it’s still on its way, it plays already, straight from your wallet.

A frame from a clip, on your card

In the card designer, My NFTs lists your video clips too. Pick one, slide to the moment you like and tap Use this frame. The still goes on your card and is kept in My pictures.

Tip the creator

When an NFT names who made it, its page has a Tip button for them.
