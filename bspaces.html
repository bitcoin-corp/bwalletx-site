bSpaces. Live Spaces for your token holders.

A bSpace is a live audio or video room inside a token room in bWalletX. The host is on stage, holders listen and watch, and anyone can raise a hand to be brought up. Hold the token and you’re in.

What a Space is

  • Live in bChatStage and audience. A host, speakers on stage and listeners. Camera is opt-in for people on stage. Runs on our own video server.
  • Live in bChatRaise hand. Listeners raise a hand, the host sees the queue and brings people up to speak.
  • Live in bChatToken-gated. A Space lives in a token room, so only holders get in. The server checks the holding, and the video server only lets in members who have joined.
  • Live since 5.1.85Spaces in bWalletX. A “Live now” banner with Join in the room, “Start a bSpace” for the issuer or a room admin, and a full-screen Space view: the stage on top, the hand queue for the host, controls at the bottom, and the room chat sliding up from below.

Paid Spaces

  • Live since 5.1.91Paid tickets. Pay at the door: you pay the host and the host’s wallet sends you the ticket. Not in the store edition.
  • ComingPaid speakers. Pay to come up on stage.
  • Live since 5.1.95Per-minute charges. Paid listening: the host sets a price, you approve a spending cap, and your wallet pays the people on stage a minute at a time. Not in the store edition.

Any fee on a paid Space will be shown before you pay. Paid Spaces will need identity and age checks before they open.

Why in the wallet

The token is the ticket, and the token is in your wallet. Putting the Space in the same place means no separate platform account and no card: you hold the token, you open the room, you join. It is the same room you already chat in.

One-way livestreams to token holders are on Live.

Not in the store edition

bWallet, the App Store and Google Play edition, has no token rooms, so it has no Spaces either.
