bMovies, inside bWalletX

bMovies is a film feed on Bitcoin SV. Scroll it like Instagram, watch thousands of films, and back the ones you want more of. Sign in with bWalletX and it works like part of your wallet.

Open bMoviesGet bWalletX

Live today Live

  • A film feed syndicated from creators on X, with around 2,300 films you can play.
  • Sign in with bWalletX (also works with bWallet). HandCash, Metanet Desktop and Yours also work for watching, liking and commenting.
  • BSV only: every payment is in Bitcoin SV, from your own wallet.
  • Film tokens: each film can have its own token. Holding it shows you backed the film.

How it fits the bApp bar Coming

Inside bWalletX, bMovies will use the same five buttons as every bApp, each one about films:

ButtonIn bMovies
WalletThe films you've backed. Your BSV balance stays in the wallet's own Wallet.
ExchangeFilm tokens, and Mint to start a film's token.
bAsk b, the wallet's assistant. Later: "make me a movie".
FeedTrending films. Like = 1¢ to back a film and join its room.
ChatEach film's room, for the people who backed it.

Coming next Coming

  • Like to fund: one tap backs a film with 1¢ (or one PNEE) and lets you into its room. Daily cap $1 by default.
  • Streaming that pays by the second: pay only for what you watch.
  • Twatch comments on films.
  • Friend movies: a film starring you and your friends. Everyone in it signs their own consent in their own wallet first, and it stays private to your group unless all of them agree to publish.
  • "b, make me a movie": ask b for a film, see the price, approve it in your wallet.

A film token is a key to that film's room and a record that you backed it. It is not an investment and doesn't pay you anything.
