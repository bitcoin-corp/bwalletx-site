Move your X and YouTube history onto Bitcoin

Almost every app lets you download everything you have put into it. FLICK OFF takes that download and writes it to Bitcoin in your own name. bWalletX signs each piece with your key and holds it in your vault.

1. Download your data→ 2. Drop it on bMintX→ 3. Own it in bWalletX

How it works

1. Download your data

Ask X or YouTube (Google Takeout) for your archive. They email you a zip. It is your data, and in the UK and EU you have a right to it.

2. Drop the zip on bMintX

The zip is read in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. You see what is inside and what it costs to mint, at cost price, before anything happens. For YouTube you pick which videos to mint.

3. Sign with bWalletX

Your wallet signs each piece and it is written to Bitcoin, keeping its original date. Your X posts land on bChatX. Your YouTube videos land on bVideoX. Your keys never leave your device.

The rules

Your own content only

FLICK OFF mints what you posted. Other people's posts are not copied. A reply to someone else links to the original.

Private messages stay private

Direct messages are counted so you know they exist. They are never minted publicly.

Cost price, shown first

You see the mint cost before you sign. It pays the Bitcoin network fee, nothing more. What you mint is a permanent public record, so check it before you sign.
