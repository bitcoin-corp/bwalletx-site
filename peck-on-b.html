On b · Using Peck with bWalletX

Peck on b

Peck posts are plain Bitcoin Schema on chain, so bWalletX can read them. Use Peck as you do, and follow it from the same place you keep your coins.

What you can do

Read Peck in the Feed

Open Feed and pick the Peck chip. Posts come from Peck’s public overlay, current to the latest block. Tap a name to see that author’s posts; tap Open to see a post on peck.to.

Tip signed authors

When a post’s signature checks out, the tip goes to the address that signed it. If it can’t be tied to the author, the tip button says so instead of guessing.

Like, reply and lock

Lock BSV behind a post you rate; it stays yours. Likes and replies you make here are written on chain as bChat posts.
