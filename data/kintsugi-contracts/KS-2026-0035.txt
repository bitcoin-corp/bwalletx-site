Terms
1. Payment is peer to peer: the client pays the developer directly, wallet to wallet. Kintsugi never holds the money.
2. Up to 8 billable hours per day.
3. Done means: the pull request is merged into the agreed repo, the change is live on the stated page, and the security tool results are attached.
4. Security tools: Shannon for any change that touches sign-in, payments, keys or signing; Semgrep, gitleaks, pnpm audit and OSV-Scanner, CodeQL, OWASP ZAP (baseline scan) and Claude Code /security-review. Tool and AI usage costs are the developer's and are not billable hours.
5. Kintsugi checks the spec and reviews the work. When the work is accepted, the client pays the developer's $handle in bWalletX, with Kintsugi's fee as a separate payment in the same transaction.
6. Disputes: if the client and developer disagree about whether the work is done, Kintsugi reviews it and decides.
7. Delivered work belongs to the client once it is paid for.
8. Code under the Open BSV licence; specs and docs CC BY 4.0.
9. All three parties sign the same contract in bChatX before work starts.
10. This log is the founder's own work, counted at up to 18 hours a day. It comes from before the 8-hour daily cap that applies to Kintsugi developers.
