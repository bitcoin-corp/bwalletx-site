What you get, and where each part stands

Every item shows where it is today. Live means it works in production now. Testing means it works, with our own company as the first user. In build means we are building it now.

Signed documents and board minutes Live

Share a contract or minute in your room. Each director reads it and signs from a computer, and the signature is sealed on chain with a record anyone can check. Signing on the phone: being fixed.

A member room with its own key Live

A private room on bChatX for your board, team or members, with its own key. Invite people with a link; they sign in with bWalletX, Google or HandCash.

A wallet in your brand Testing

Our branded wallet edition can be part of your build: your name, colours and logo, self-custody for every user. The first client edition is in trial.

Payments, tips and receipts Live

Take payments in BSV through bWalletX, with a receipt for each one that anyone can check.

Brand, website and launch film Live

Logo, colours, type and voice, a website that works with your wallet, share images and a short launch film, made at bAdvertisingX.

Your team's wallet on a big screen Live

bWalletX Desktop in the browser: money, chat and documents side by side, installable as an app.

Your share register Testing

Your register of members and cap table kept with bSharesX. Registers are in testing; the first one is our own company's.

The Enterprise desktop app In build

A signed Mac app for running a company: many accounts, everyday accounts kept apart from the treasury, multisig, approvals for each account, an audit log, and registers for keys, domains and email.

Private code snapshots on chain In build

Seal a snapshot of your code on chain, encrypted so only your wallet can open it, through bGitX.
