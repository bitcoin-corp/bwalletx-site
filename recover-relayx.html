How to find them

  1. Open bWalletX and scroll to the bottom of your wallet.
  2. Under Bring in old assets, tap Recover RelayX collections.
  3. Enter your RelayX 12 words. Paste them however you saved them: spaces, commas or a numbered list all work.
  4. Tap Find my NFTs. bWalletX reads the chain for each of your RelayX addresses and counts up what it finds.
  5. Your NFTs appear as a gallery, grouped by collection.
  6. Tap one to see it large: its number, collection, and the address that holds it.

Ninja Punk Girls

Ninja Punk Girls was one of the largest collections on RelayX. Every one of them is now identified:

  • Series 2: all 3,333 NFTs, each matched to her card number and picture. RelayX stored the pictures encrypted, so most were matched by the exact size of each minted file, and the rest by mint order between those matches.
  • Series 1: all 25 NFTs, identified by name (13 girls).
  • Single-girl collections such as KAZUYO and AIKA are named from the collection itself.

Melting a girl into her element cards

Each NPG is built from layered element cards: hair, face, outfit, weapon and more. If you hold one, you can melt her into those cards for the NPG games, plus her round avatar badge, which only someone who melted an original can hold.

  • One approval. A single sheet shows her, the cards she becomes, and the fees.
  • Fees at cost. About 1¢ for the network and minting. No melt fee.
  • The old NFT is burned on chain, in a way anyone can check. That is what makes the new cards hers and nobody else’s.
  • Series 1 melts into the redrawn element set for that girl: the same girl, redrawn as parts, not the original artwork.

Safety

  • Your words never leave your phone. bWalletX reads public chain data only. Nothing about your keys is sent to us or to anyone.
  • Finding is read-only. The scan moves nothing.
  • Melting is the only action, and it is one deliberate tap on one NFT.
  • RelayX NFTs are never swept. bWalletX recognises them before anything else and won’t spend them as coins.
