bShares.
Any company’s register, on chain.

A register of members, kept by the company. Shares issued as tokens, approved by your directors, and locked in each holder’s wallet so the company and the holder both have a say.

In testing bShares is a record-keeping tool for any company. It’s in testing; the first register is our own company, The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. It isn’t open to other companies yet. Nothing on this page is an offer of shares, and no shares are sold here or to the public.

For any company

bShares is a bApp in bWalletX for companies of any size. The same tools are being shaped so any company can keep its register, its directors and its members’ holdings here.

The register is the record

The register of members is the legal title. bShares reads holdings from it, never from a list in the app. Certificate numbers are never reused.

Directors approve every allotment

A board minute is drafted in the company’s own format, and a director approves it before any share is issued. An allotment that would go over the company’s authorised shares is refused.

A share with two keys

Each holding is a token in the holder’s wallet, locked 2-of-2: one key for the director, one for the holder. Nobody can move it alone.

And holders never depend on us. After a set block height, the holder can move their token alone, from their own seed. No server, no director needed.

Private placements

A company can invite people it already knows to buy shares privately, under its own rules and the law where it is registered; bShares only keeps the record. A member pays in BSV to a 2-of-3 company treasury, with director keys on separate devices. Pay too much and you choose: a refund within 14 days, or treat it as share premium.

What works today

  • BuiltRegister of members, phase 1. A company’s register and its directors’ approvals, in private testing.
  • BuiltTwo kinds of lock. The company’s money sits in a 2-of-3 treasury on its directors’ keys (for one director: phone, desktop and an offline backup). Each member’s shares are locked 2-of-2 to the company and the member, with a timed way out the member can use alone.
  • BuiltIn the Vault. Holdings show in the Lock vault as Restricted, not for sale.
  • BuiltExchange “Shares” filter. Holdings appear marked Restricted. There are no buy or sell buttons.
  • ComingSet-up for any company. Your own name and ticker, import your register, add director keys, use your minute template.
  • ComingPartnership shares stage. Friends start something together before there’s a company. See Partnership shares.
  • Liveb drafts in partner chats. Ask b in the partners’ chat to draft an agreement or summarise the terms; every partner accepts before it counts.
  • ComingIncorporate 1:1. Turn partnership shares into company shares, one for one.

What bShares is not

It isn’t a place to buy shares. No shares are offered or sold on this site or in bWalletX, and bShares is left out of bWallet, the App Store and Google Play builds. It’s a tool for a company to keep its own records and lock its members’ holdings.
