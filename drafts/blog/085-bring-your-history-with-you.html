Bring your history with you

Start bWalletX with the account you already use: X, Google, GitHub, LinkedIn or HandCash. Your name, photo and a $name come with you. So can the years of posts behind them, kept in your own wallet, private unless you choose otherwise, for as long as you want them.

Start with an account you already have

You don’t need to invent a new identity to use bWalletX. On the first screen you can link the account you already use most: X, Google, GitHub, LinkedIn or HandCash. Link one, or several.

Linking is not handing anything over. The provider confirms the account is yours, and your wallet signs a note saying so. bWalletX never gets your password, and the provider never gets your wallet keys. For Google we keep a tick that says your email is verified, and that’s all.

Your name, your photo, your $name

Your display name and photo come across from the account you linked, so you look like you from the first minute. You can change either whenever you like.

You also get a $name: a short paymail like name@bwalletx.com that friends can pay instead of a long address. If you linked X, your X handle is offered first. Each linked account can suggest a name of its own, like name.x or name.github, and that name is only ever given to the person who proved they own that account. Pick as many as you like and choose one to show.

Bring your history

Your posts belong to you. You wrote them over years, and they say something about who you are. Now you can keep them.

On bChatX, bchatx.com/migrate/x takes the archive X lets you download. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Every post keeps its date and a link back to the original, and is minted to your own wallet in one approval. If you close the tab, it picks up where it left off. Nothing is posted to X and nothing there is deleted.

Private by default, public by one tap

Everything you bring in is encrypted to your wallet. Only you can open it. That’s the default for anything you mint in bWalletX, not only your posts.

If you want the world to see something, Make public now is one tap away, and you can make anything public later. Making something public can’t be undone, so we leave that choice with you.

Yours, for good

Your history doesn’t sit on our servers waiting for us to decide what happens to it. It’s recorded on Bitcoin in your name, and your 12 words open it. If we disappeared tomorrow, your records would still be there, and still yours.

Because it’s yours, you can pass it on. In bWalletX, bTrust lets you split your recovery into slices for people you trust and plan who receives your wallet, with check-ins while you’re around and a Claim an inheritance screen for your heirs. Whatever your wallet holds, including the private history you brought with you, goes with it.
