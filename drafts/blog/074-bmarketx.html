bMarketX: your eBay store, on Bitcoin

bMarketX.com gives your eBay shop a second home. Move it in about a minute, and Buy It Now pays you straight to your wallet.

A store page you already know

Every shop gets a page laid out the way eBay buyers know: banner, feedback, listings in a grid, prices in dollars.

Move in about a minute

Sign in with eBay and import your listings, upload the listings file from Seller Hub, or paste your store link and prove it’s yours with a short code.

Paid straight to your wallet

Your bWalletX is your account: no password to choose. Buy It Now pays your wallet directly, with no fee from us, and buyers can reach you by bMail. If you like, mint the whole shop: each listing becomes a token in your wallet and the supply is your stock.
