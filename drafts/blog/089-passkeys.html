Sign in with your face or fingerprint

bWalletX works with passkeys: Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN. Your 12 words are sealed with the passkey, so you can make a wallet and come back to it without copying anything down first.

One button

The welcome screen leads with Continue with a passkey and tells you where the passkey is kept: your Apple or Google account. If you prefer, a password and your 12 words still work exactly as before.

A way back in on a new phone

Your 12 words are sealed with the passkey and saved as a recovery file you keep, plus a locked copy so your passkey alone brings your wallet to a new phone. Only your passkey opens it.

Want to see the words anyway? Show my 12 words asks for your passkey. Chrome, Edge and Android can also save the wallet’s password in your password manager as a second way back in.

In the Chrome extension

In the extension, Continue with a passkey opens web.bwalletx.com. Use your passkey there and type the six digits it shows into the extension. Your wallet comes across, and you set a password for the extension, with a strong one offered for you to save.

Where it works

Passkeys work on web.bwalletx.com, in the Android download and in the Chrome extension.
