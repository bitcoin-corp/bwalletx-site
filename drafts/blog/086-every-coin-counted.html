Development blog · 086 · 30 Oct 2026

Every coin, counted

Your balance is always the whole picture. bWalletX checks the chain and your own backups, so everything you own shows up, automatically, on every device you use.

Checked against the chain

When you unlock, bWalletX quietly compares what it shows with what the chain says, and keeps checking while you use it. Coins you spent from another device drop off; coins that arrived show up. Payments to your $name appear within seconds.

Your backups, read too

Your encrypted backups are part of the picture. On a new phone, bWalletX reads your backup before it goes live, so your history and every payment to your $name come with you. Rescan the chain in Settings › Storage makes sure your balance includes everything you own: it reads the chain and every backup the wallet holds, with one tap.

One green button

Want a fresh look? REFRESH WALLET at the bottom of Home syncs, re-checks every coin and shows a checklist as it goes. It never sends anything.

Any payment, by its ID

Know a payment happened? Paste its transaction ID in Settings › Troubleshooting and it’s added to your wallet. Every row in History has Copy ID.

Sends that just go

Use the same wallet on your phone and your laptop and sends still go first time: the wallet uses only coins the chain confirms are yours, and finishes every payment it starts.
