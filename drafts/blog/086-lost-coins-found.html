When coins go missing, the wallet goes looking

Through October a handful of people saw a balance that looked wrong. Each time we found where the money was, and taught the wallet to find it on its own. Here is what changed, and the one green button to press if your balance ever looks off.

Press REFRESH WALLET

At the bottom of Home there is a green REFRESH WALLET button. It syncs, re-checks your coins and looks for missing ones, and shows a checklist as it goes. It never sends anything. If your balance seems wrong, start there.

Find missing coins

Find missing coins in Settings › Storage reads the wallet’s store and every backup it holds. It lists change that was mined but that the wallet had lost track of, and restores it after one confirm. If it can’t read everything, it says so instead of telling you nothing was found.

Recover a payment by its ID

If you know a payment happened, you can bring it back by its transaction ID in Settings › Troubleshooting. History has a Copy ID button next to each transaction for exactly this.

Coins spent on another device

Use the same wallet on two devices and one of them can think it still holds coins the other already spent. That made sends fail. Now the wallet quietly checks after you unlock, clears only coins whose spend is confirmed, and if a send still hits one, clears it and tries once more, or tells you plainly to tap Send again.

Payments we should not have dropped

In one case our server sent a payment and then answered with an error, and the wallet gave up on it. Now the wallet only cancels a payment when the server refused it before sending and the chain still can’t see it after about 30 seconds. Otherwise it waits and finishes the payment.

Restoring on a new device now checks your encrypted backup before using it, payments received at your $name come back into History, and sweeps that were mined but hidden are found and shown once they can be spent.
