BlastPad: launch a coin in one transaction

BlastPad on Tokenblaster.lol launches a coin from your wallet in one transaction, with its own page and short link.

Launch

Add a square image, name your coin and connect your wallet. One transaction inscribes the image and mints 1,000,000,000 tokens onto the coin’s curve. Nothing is set aside: even your own first buy is on the curve. Each coin shows who launched it.

A page for every coin

Coins are listed at tokenblaster.lol/tokens, each with a page and a short link.

In your wallet

New coins appear in Exchange › Memepool, each with a room and a store. See 066.
