Bring your X posts, in three steps

bchatx.com/migrate/x brings your X archive home. Every post keeps its date, lands in your own wallet, and stays private until you show it.

Three steps

Ask X for your archive. When X emails you, download the .zip. Pick it on bChatX and press Mint. Most of the time is just waiting for X.

Read on your device

The .zip is read in your browser and never uploaded. Every post keeps its date and a link back to the original.

Yours, and private

Each post is minted to your own wallet in one approval, and only you can see it until you choose to show it. Close the tab and it picks up where it left off. Nothing is posted to X and nothing there is deleted.

Know someone with years of posts on X? The page has an invite you can send them.
