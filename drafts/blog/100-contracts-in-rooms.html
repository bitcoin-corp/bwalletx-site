Contracts, right in the room

Draft a contract with b in any bChatX room, send it as an offer, and sign with your wallet. A sale settles the moment it’s accepted: goods and payment change hands together.

Ask b to write it

Type $b draft a contract… and b writes a first draft with you. Drafts sync across your devices, and every contract you’ve made sits in one record.

Offer, then sign

Send the contract as an offer. Each version is encrypted end to end and signed, so everyone can see exactly what changed. Counter-sign when you agree.

Sales that settle themselves

For a sale, accepting the contract swaps the tokens and the payment in one transaction through bWalletX. Nobody has to go first.
