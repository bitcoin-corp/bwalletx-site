Meetings in bChatX

Private rooms can book a meeting or meet right now. Invites go out as you book, and the letter in bMail shows Join meeting when it’s time.

Book or meet now

A private room has a Book a meeting banner. Pick a time on the calendar, invite people as you book, or tap Meet now.

Join from bMail

The invite arrives in bMail and shows Join meeting from ten minutes before. The notification opens the room if it’s live.
