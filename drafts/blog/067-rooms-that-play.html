Rooms that play

Game rooms in bChatX now have a big PLAY button, a floating player you can drag anywhere, and GO LIVE to stream your game to the room.

Play without leaving

Tap PLAY in a game room’s header and the game opens in a floating player. Drag it to any corner and keep chatting while you play.

Watch together

Room video floats the same way. Tap the pill to bring it back.

GO LIVE

Stream the game you’re playing into the room, with up to 16 players live at once.
