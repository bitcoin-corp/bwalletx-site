Names that can’t pretend

bWalletX and bChatX flag look-alike and brand names, keep provider names for the people who prove them, and warn you before you trust someone posing as someone else.

Look-alikes flagged

Names that imitate another, like a 0 for an o, are flagged in the wallet, in bChatX and on bMail senders. Brand names and very short names are kept back.

Bots and rooms too

Bots must say who runs them, room names follow the same brand rules, and suspected phishing links carry a warning.
