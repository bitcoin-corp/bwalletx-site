Sign on your phone

Sign documents with your finger on your phone. Scan the QR on your computer, or tap the notification, and sign in bWalletX.

Scan and sign

A document on your computer shows Sign here and a QR. Scan it with your phone, bWalletX opens, checks the document matches, and you draw your signature.

Or tap the notification

When someone asks you to sign, the notification names the document and opens Review & sign. If you’re on the wrong account, it tells you.
