bGitX: your code, on b

Connect your GitHub to bGitX and each repo gets a room for the people who work on it. Kintsugi can open pull requests, and nothing merges without your OK.

Connect your repos

Connect bWalletX, then GitHub. You choose which repos, through your own GitHub App install.

A room per repo

Each repo gets a bChatX room for the people working on it, and all your repos sit on one page.

Fixes as pull requests

Kintsugi, our repair agent, fixes and builds by opening pull requests. You read them; nothing merges without you.
