Your money, in plain words

History shows every payment with what it was worth on the day, totals for any dates, and statements to print or export. And every send ends on a cheerful Sent!

Every payment, in plain words

Tap History under your wallet card. Each transaction is marked in, out or to yourself, with the fee, who it was with, and what it was worth in dollars that day. Token and NFT buys, games, subscriptions and app payments are named, and chips let you filter by kind.

Refresh shows that it’s working and when it last updated, and each row has Copy ID.

Totals and statements

Choose a date range to see totals, fees, and your opening and closing balance. Export a CSV for a spreadsheet, or a statement you can print or save as a PDF.

Who has been calling

The Connections tab lists the sites and apps that used your wallet and what they were paid, with a way to cut each one off.

Sent!

Every successful send ends on a Sent! screen with the amount, who got it and a link to see it on chain, plus a short coin chime. It follows your theme. You can turn the sound off in Settings.
