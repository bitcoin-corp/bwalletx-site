Tokenblaster.lol and WELCOME TO TERRORNODE

Tokenblaster.lol is an arcade on the live BSV chain: every shot is a real transaction. Its biggest game, WELCOME TO TERRORNODE, plays in teams, in 64-player melees and full screen on phones.

An arcade on the chain

Shoot your own tokens in Arena, ride Block Hopper where every transaction is ground, or race bRacer through live traffic.

WELCOME TO TERRORNODE

A rail shooter set in Neo-Tokyo, 2048. LOCK & LOAD asks your wallet once, then play in teams, in a 64-player MELEE, or watch as a spectator. On a phone, turn it sideways for full screen.

From your wallet

bWalletX has a TERRORNODE tile on Home, and its room and shop open the game. Tokenblaster.lol uses one wallet for the whole site and pairs with your phone with a signed check.
