Pay artists by the second

Echoes in the Abyss by VexVoid is in bWalletX. The first six tracks are free, the rest cost 1 sat a second, and the money goes straight to the artist.

One album, twelve tracks

Open Media in bWalletX Desktop and the album is on the front shelf, with a big square cover. Track 1 is free. Tracks 2 to 6 are sponsored: VexVoid pays for your listening from a pot, so you hear them free too.

Tracks 7 to 12 cost 1 sat for each second you listen. Stop when you like and you stop paying. Every sat goes to the artist's wallet as you listen, not weeks later.

The listener board

Fans can opt in to a board of top listeners, with their rank and badges. It is your choice to join, and you can opt out again at any time.

For artists

Press "Add a release" in bWalletX Desktop to publish your own music or video with a square cover, on chain. Listeners pay you by the second, and you can sponsor them from a pot so they hear you free.

The new page at bwalletx.com/artists shows the album and how to start.
