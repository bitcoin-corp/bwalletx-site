bRadio: live audio in bWalletX

Host a live audio Space, bring people up to speak, and keep listening while you use the rest of the wallet. Recorded Spaces can become podcast episodes.

Go live

Start a Space and you’re the host. People join to listen. Anyone can raise a hand, and you bring them up to the stage to speak. Whoever is talking gets a gold ring round their bAvatar, so you always know who’s speaking.

When someone you follow goes live, you get a notification: “$name is hosting”. Tap it and you’re in.

Keep listening

Leave the Space screen and it keeps playing. A live bar sits at the top and a mini player at the bottom, so you can pay, chat or browse and still hear the room. Tap either one to go back in.

The bRadio Lounge

The bRadio Lounge is the place to find people talking and to try it out. Drop in, listen, raise a hand.

Podcasts from Spaces

A recorded Space can become a podcast episode. Anyone can play a free preview. This is off by default: nothing is recorded or published unless the host turns it on.

An episode can also be an NFT. Whoever owns it can listen to the whole thing.
