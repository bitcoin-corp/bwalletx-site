Your NFTs, edge to edge

bWalletX Desktop has an NFT library. Every picture, clip and song you own is in one grid, opens edge to edge, and a moment from a video can become the art on your card.

One library

On Desktop, go to Wallet → NFTs (desktop.bwalletx.com/wallet/nfts). Everything you own is in one grid, with a preview for each kind: pictures, video, music and documents. Filter by kind, open a collection, or search. NFTs that are still on their way say so.

Tickets and credits have their own tabs next to it, so the grid is just your things.

Edge to edge

Open an NFT and the picture or clip fills the space in its own shape. There is no frame around it: the screen is the frame. Video plays where it is, and the details and actions sit around it, not on top of it.

If you’ve just been sent an NFT and it’s still on its way, it plays already, straight from your wallet.

A frame from a clip, on your card

In the card designer, My NFTs now lists your video clips too. Pick one, slide to the moment you like, and tap Use this frame. That still goes on your card and is kept in My pictures.

Tip the creator

When an NFT names who made it, its page has a Tip button for them.
