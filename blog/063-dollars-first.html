Dollars first, and fewer prompts

Every approval and payment sheet now shows the amount in dollars first, with sats small underneath. And everyday use asks you far less often.

Prices you can read

Whether you’re paying a friend, a site or a shop, the sheet leads with dollars. The exact sats sit underneath for anyone who wants them.

Asked once, not every time

Sites you use often stop interrupting you. If you disconnect a site or stay away for a day, its next request asks Reconnect to <site>?, never in the middle of what you’re doing.
