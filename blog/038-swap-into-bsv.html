Swap into BSV

Most people arrive holding something other than BSV. The Swap into BSV card on the bWalletX home screen turns it into BSV in your own wallet.

How it works

  1. Pick the coin you have, from a list of 1,000+ with search and the popular ones first.
  2. Enter an amount and see about how much BSV you get.
  3. Get a deposit address and send your coin to it from wherever it is now.
  4. Track it: deposit seen, swapping, sending BSV, in your wallet. You can leave the screen; it carries on.

The swap is run by ChangeNOW, not by bWalletX, and the BSV goes straight to your own wallet address. We never hold your coins. ChangeNOW may pause a swap for checks and ask for details before releasing or refunding it. Rates float, so the amount you get can differ a little from the first quote.

A permanent card

The swap card used to have an × to hide it, and then there was no way to bring it back. It is now always on the home screen, and anyone who hid it gets it back. There is also a page about it at bwalletx.com/swap.

Swaps are in bWalletX, not in the App Store and Google Play edition.

Where it is

Live on web.bwalletx.com and the desktop beta at beta.bwalletx.com.
