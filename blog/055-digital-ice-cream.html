Digital Ice-Cream

A story about three friends, an ice-cream stand and digital cash.

Fred, Joe and You

Fred, Joe and You open an ice-cream stand. Real ice cream, in real cones. Customers pay in digital cash from their phones.

They agree up front how the takings are split between the three of them. Every sale is split the moment it’s paid. Nobody does the sums at the end of the month, and nobody has to trust whoever holds the till.

The float stays put

The stand’s float sits behind a lock that takes more than one of them to move, so no one can quietly walk off with it.

Their own room

The three of them talk in their own private chat, a room only they can get into.

What works today

  • Automatic splits. Payments split between people the moment they arrive.
  • Multisig locks. Funds that take more than one person to move.
  • Token rooms. A private chat for the people who hold a token.

Update, 30 Oct 2026: we have cut this post back to what works today and taken out a section on partnership shares, which aren’t live.
