NFTs that just work

Sending an NFT is now two steps. NFTs sent to you say who sent them, open in a viewer, and wait for you to keep them. Video NFTs show their cover, and duplicates can be burned.

Send in two steps

Choose "Who gets it?", then press one Send button. That's all. If a send does fail, the wallet tells you why.

When someone sends you one

NFTs sent to you show who sent them. Tap one to open it in a viewer. If you want it, press Keep and it moves into your NFTs. If not, leave it where it is.

Video covers

Video NFTs show their cover art in the list, and they play on iPhone too.

Burn the duplicates

If you have more copies than you want, open one and choose Burn. You confirm by typing BURN or holding the button for two seconds. If it is your only copy, the wallet warns you first.
