Your RelayX NFTs, home again

If you collected NFTs on RelayX, bWalletX can find them. Restore your RelayX wallet or type its 12 words, and your collection appears as it’s found, grouped by collection. Nothing moves unless you ask.

Find your collection

Restore a RelayX wallet into bWalletX and a blue Your RelayX collection card appears at the top. One tap finds its NFTs. Or use the RelayX importer and enter your RelayX words: they stay on your phone and aren’t stored.

Next to everything else

The NFTs tab has an All · 1Sat · Run filter, so your RelayX pieces sit beside your other NFTs. Ninja Punk Girls show as their full cards. NFTs still listed for sale on RelayX are found too, and one tap cancels the listing back to your own address.

Kept safe

Your RelayX NFTs stay NFTs: they’re never swept or spent as plain BSV. BSV on old RelayX addresses moves into your account in one tap.
