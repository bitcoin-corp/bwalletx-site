A card for every balance, and an address book

Swipe the wallet card to see each balance on a card of its own. Your contacts live in a proper address book, and Send shows exactly who you’re paying before any money moves.

Swipe through your money

The wallet card swipes to Tokens, bTickets, bShares, bCredits, MNEE, PNEEs and Top up, each with its real balance. No sample numbers. Swiping to a card filters the list under it, and each card turns over to explain what it holds. Press and hold for All cards.

An address book

Your contacts sit in an address book. A contact’s card shows only what they’ve published or can prove, plus private notes that stay on your device. You can import and export contacts. New people show by their name, never as “Anonymous”.

Who, then how much

Send opens on Who are you paying? with Paste, Scan and Contacts, and shows the real name and paymail of the person you picked. Then you choose the amount.

Before you confirm, a paymail payment shows the person’s name, bAvatar and key fingerprint. The first time you pay someone, it says so.

Smooth from the first tap

Every screen opens cleanly, and if one ever can’t draw, you get a calm screen with Reload instead of a blank page.
