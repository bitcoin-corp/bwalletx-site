The Store: $1 themes, and a receipt that proves it

The Store sells wallet themes for $1, paid in BSV with one approval. Each purchase comes with a receipt signed by the store, and the themes you buy work across all your accounts.

A mall of shops

Exchange › Store is laid out as shops, each with its own banner and logo. NPG, VexVoid, NPGX and TokenBlaster all have themes, and there are twelve emoji themes that carry the emoji’s colour through the app and the emoji on your card.

$1, one approval, your choice

A theme costs $1, network fee included, in one approval sheet. Nothing switches on by itself: you choose Apply now or Done. Bought themes show their art on your card and sit in a Themes tab.

A receipt you keep

The same payment puts a receipt NFT in your wallet, signed by the store. It’s your proof of purchase, and from the receipt you can switch the theme on again any time.

Free looks too

Plenty of looks cost nothing: White, Pink, Blue and Yellow paper, Mono, Duo and Tri, and a colour mixer with Shuffle.
