Chats that feel like Telegram

Chats in bWalletX open straight away and the list stays still. Swipe a chat to pin, mute, archive or mark it read.

Instant

Tap a chat and it opens. The list no longer jumps about while it loads.

Swipe to sort

Swipe a chat to pin it to the top, mute it, archive it or mark it read, the way Telegram does it.

Archived, out of the way

Archived chats go into one Archived row at the top of the list. Tap it to open them, and the rest of your list stays clear.
