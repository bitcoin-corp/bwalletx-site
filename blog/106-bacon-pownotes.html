Bacon PoWNotes

Bacon Notes are one-of-a-kind PoWNotes that hold their own $BACON, a thank-you for bringing friends to bChatX. Nothing is minted yet: here is the plan, and what works today.

Six degrees

Your X profile is already on bChatX at bchatx.com/x/<handle>. Claim it by signing in with X, and you can bring 7 friends free.

$BACON and Bacon Notes

$BACON is a BSV-21 memecoin, 1,000,000,000 supply. You get $BACON for each friend who claims their profile, and a Bacon Note for every 6. Each note has a pig portrait, rasher guilloche, a serial number and a face value of 1K, 10K, 100K or 1M $BACON, with rare golden and misprint notes. Each one holds its $BACON.

How a PoWNote works

  • It holds its value. Each note is a 1Sat NFT, and its coin holds the note’s face value.
  • Send it like cash. When you send a note, the person you send it to gets a new edition, and you keep a shell with no value in it.
  • Melt it. Melting a note returns the value it holds.
  • Yours alone. Once a note is issued, only the holder controls it. We cannot move it for you.

How you know a note is genuine

Official PoWNotes, including Bacon Notes, are issued only by The Bitcoin Corporation Mint. That is how you know they are genuine.

  • The Mint signs each note’s origin, serial and face value together.
  • A note is genuine only if its on-chain origin is in the Mint’s signed registry.
  • Copies and duplicate serials are refused.
  • Fakes are never delivered, never shown as genuine and never listed on our markets. If one is shown at all, it carries the label “Not issued by The Bitcoin Corporation Mint”.

Safe pictures

We draw every note ourselves, using only shapes, colours and text, with no code inside. Notes are always shown as pictures.
