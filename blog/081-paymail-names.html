Your $name: free, yours, and easy to pay

Your $name is a paymail people pay instead of a long address. It’s free, works at bwalletx.com and bmailx.com, and is signed by your own wallet.

Free and instant

Tap Get your $name and choose a handle. It works as name@bwalletx.com and name@bmailx.com. Change it in Settings › Identity and your old name keeps receiving.

Your X handle

When you create an account you can link X and use your X handle as your $name.

Signed by you

Every name is signed by its owner’s wallet, with a public log of changes, and Send warns you before a first payment to a look-alike. See 078.
