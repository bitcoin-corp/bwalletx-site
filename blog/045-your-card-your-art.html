Your card, your art

The card in bWalletX can wear your own pictures or your NFTs, with standard colour sliders. And bPics lets you mint a picture from your phone.

Design your card

The card designer now has standard colour sliders. Pick "My pictures" to use a photo, or "My NFTs" to put one of your NFTs on your card. A swivel icon on the back flips it round again.

bPics

bPics is new. Take or choose a picture and mint it, so it is yours on chain. You can share it in a public library, or keep it to yourself. Sharing is your choice, picture by picture.
