Ask b

b, the helper inside bWalletX, has a calmer home. Hold the gold b to talk, and your chats on bChatX now have their own links and are encrypted with your wallet.

A quiet place to ask

b opens on a dark pool with a few gentle suggestions drifting past: a savings pot, sending money to someone, pocket money. Or just type your own question.

Hold to talk

Hold the gold b and speak. Your words appear in the box, and nothing sends until you do. b can fill in a payment for you, but nothing goes until you say so.

Your chats with b, kept private

On bChatX, Ask b lives at bchatx.com/ask-b. Each chat has its own link, so you can come back to it. Pin the ones you use and hide the ones you don't; opening hidden chats asks your wallet to sign first.

With bWalletX connected, your chats are encrypted by your wallet before they are saved. We store the locked copy, not the words.

What's not done

  • Each message you send b still goes to the AI provider to be answered, in plain text, so it can reply.
