Development blog · 046 · 12 Oct 2026

Stays unlocked, sends exactly

bWalletX no longer locks itself after a few minutes, and fingerprint unlock on Android works again. Token sends show the exact amount before anything is paid.

No more surprise locks

Auto-lock now defaults to Never. You can still lock the wallet yourself, or set a timer in Settings if you prefer one.

Fingerprint on Android

Fingerprint unlock works again on Android. If it ever needs setting up again, the wallet asks you.

Token sends you can check

Before a token send pays the indexer, you see the exact amount and confirm it. If your wallet is out of date for that token, one tap on "Fix and send" puts it right and sends.
