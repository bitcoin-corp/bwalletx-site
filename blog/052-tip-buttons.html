Tip buttons

bWalletX Desktop has a Tip button for artists, NFT creators, rooms, people in chat, friends and speakers in Spaces. Pick an amount, tap Allow once, and all of it goes to them.

Where you’ll find it

  • bMusic: the artist page, each track, and the album player.
  • NFTs: the creator, when the NFT names one.
  • Chat: the room’s creator in the header, and each person by their name.
  • Friends: on each friend’s page.
  • Spaces: each speaker on stage.

You never see a Tip button on your own things.

One sheet, one Allow

Pick 10p, 50p, £1 or £5 (or 10¢, 50¢, $1, $5), or type your own amount, and add a note if you like. bWalletX works out the BSV at today’s price. Tap Allow once.

All of it goes to them

The tip goes from your wallet to theirs. We take nothing. If the network is slow, the wallet tells you it’s on its way, so you don’t pay twice.
