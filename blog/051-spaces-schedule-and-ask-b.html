Spaces on a schedule, b on every page

Spaces in bWalletX Desktop have a weekly schedule in your own time zone, with Add to calendar. And Ask b is now a small bar at the bottom of every page.

Featured Spaces

The Spaces page opens with a Featured row: the Lounge first, then public Spaces that are live right now. Tap Join to go in.

Regular Spaces

Below it is the week’s schedule of regular Spaces, shown in your own local time. Each one has Add to calendar, which saves it to your calendar app, and a stable link to its room.

Ask b, on every page

The gold b is now a slim bar at the bottom of every Desktop page. Type a question and b’s answer floats above the bar; close it when you’re done. Press / or ⌘K to start typing. b knows which screen you’re on, so “what does this do?” works.

Not sure what to ask? The Ask b page cycles through around a hundred things b can help with, in a new order each visit. Tap one and it fills in the bar; nothing is sent until you press Send. Open full chat takes your conversation there.

Calls is now bPhone

Calls has a new name on Desktop: bPhone, at /bphone. Old links still work.
