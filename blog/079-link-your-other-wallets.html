Link your other wallets

Prove you own an Ethereum, BNB, Solana or TRON wallet and show it on your profile, signed and checkable.

One profile, every chain

In Settings › Linked wallets, sign a message with your other wallet. bWalletX records the proof, and your profile shows the link.

Nothing moves

Linking proves ownership only. No coins move and no keys are shared.
