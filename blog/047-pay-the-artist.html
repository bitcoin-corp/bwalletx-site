Pay the artist

bMusic is on bWalletX Desktop. Anyone can open an artist page and hear the first track without signing in. The next tracks are paid for by the artist, and after that your money goes straight to them.

Artists in bMusic

Open desktop.bwalletx.com/bmusic/artists and pick an artist. VexVoid is first. The artist page has a short bio, every track, a video, the price of the originals, and the people who have listened most.

Try before you sign up

You don’t need a wallet to look around. Track 1 plays straight away, with no account. If you like it, bWalletX offers to make you a wallet in a minute, and the next few tracks are paid for by the artist’s own pot. You don’t pay for those.

For the rest of the album, bWalletX shows you how to add a little BSV: swap another coin into it, or receive some from a friend. Then you carry on where you left off.

Paid by the second, paid to the artist

Paid tracks cost 1 sat a second while they play. Stop the track and the payment stops. The money goes from your wallet to the artist. Nobody takes a cut in the middle.

A Tip button too

The artist page, each track and the album player all have a Tip button. Pick 10p, 50p, £1, £5 or your own amount, then tap Allow once. All of it goes to the artist.
