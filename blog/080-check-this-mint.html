Check this mint lets anyone verify a mint in their own browser, without trusting us. And bVideoX keeps turning YouTube libraries into records in your own name.

Check this mint

Paste a bMintX link or transaction ID at bmintx.com/check or in bWalletX. Your browser checks the signature, the proof and the block itself, and answers VERIFIED, PENDING, REFUSED or UNREACHABLE. Drop in the original file and it checks that too, without uploading it.
