Partnership accounts

Running something with partners? Set it up in bWalletX. Everyone signs the agreement in their own wallet, money coming in splits by share automatically, and a shared account needs your partners’ approval to spend.

Set up your partners

Add your partners and their shares, and b drafts the agreement. Each partner signs it in their own wallet, so everyone holds the same signed copy.

Money splits itself

Payments to the partnership split by share, straight to each partner’s wallet. Nobody has to divide anything by hand at the end of the month.

Correction, 10 Nov 2026: splitting payments by share wasn’t part of 5.2.32 and isn’t available yet.

A shared account

Keep BSV together in a shared account that needs several partners to approve each payment. Any partner can propose one; the others approve it from their own wallet, and the wallet signs only a payment that matches the card everyone saw.

Between you

Partnership shares belong to the partners. They’re held under multi-signature and aren’t traded on any public market. bWalletX gives you the tools; we don’t sell or promote shares.
