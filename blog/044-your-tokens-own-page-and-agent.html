Your token's own page and agent

Every token on bChatX has a full page: price, chart, holders, posts and a Buy button. Its creator can give it an agent, and holders can show a badge name.

A page for every token

Open a token, like bchatx.com/t/VEXVOID, to see the token's price and chart, its holders, whether its Space is live, its posts and a Buy button. Tap any avatar to see it full screen. The pages also have their own share cards.

Token agents

A token's creator can give it an agent with a name ending in .agent, like vexvoid.agent. It greets people, welcomes new holders and answers questions about the token in its room, using what was said there recently. It never talks about price.

Names with a dot are reserved for these, so nobody can claim a lookalike.

Badge names and members

In a token room your name shows with the token beside it, like john.frogger, while you hold it. Holders can choose to appear in the token's members list, and choose who may message them: any holder, only people they've chatted with, or nobody. A first message arrives as a request you can accept.
