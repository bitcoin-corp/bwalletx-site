Every launch gets a fair curve

Every token you launch in bWalletX gets a launch curve, written on chain when you mint: 1 billion tokens, the first at 1¢, and a height you choose.

Pick a height

Choose how far the price climbs: Standard (to 19¢), Big (to $1.99) or Huge (to $19.99). The first 1% is an early tier capped at $10 per person, so a launch can’t be swallowed in one go.

Fixed and checkable

The curve is written on chain at mint, can’t be changed, and has a fingerprint anyone can check. You can choose to burn unsold tokens after 6 or 12 months, or never.

A page and a market

Every token gets a page with its curve chart, holders and a link to its room. List tokens for sale in dollars from the token row, and buying is one tap.
