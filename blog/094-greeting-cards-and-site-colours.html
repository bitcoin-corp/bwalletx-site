Greeting cards, and sites in their own colours

Mint can make a greeting card or an AI picture and send it to a $name. And sites you connect to can show their own colours and logo on approval sheets, while the wallet always shows the real address.

Greeting cards

In Mint, choose Greeting card or AI picture. Pick an occasion, say who it’s for, write what you want and choose a model. You see the price before you pay, and your card appears as it’s made. Then Send it delivers it to a $name as an NFT.

You always see the price first, and you only pay for a card that’s made.

Sites in their own colours

A site you connect to can publish its colours, name and logo, and its approval sheets wear them. A wallet strip above always shows the true site address, and the important parts (amounts, who gets paid, the Approve button) are never themed. You can turn site themes off in Settings.
