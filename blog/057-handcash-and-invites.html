Sign in with HandCash, and invites with tokens

New accounts can now start with HandCash as well as X. Your HandCash handle carries over as name@bwalletx.com, your friends can come with you, and invites with tokens bring people into Spaces.

Sign in with HandCash

“Sign in with HandCash” now sits next to “Sign in with X” when you make a new account. If you already use HandCash, you don’t need to start from nothing.

  • Your handle carries over. Your HandCash handle becomes your paymail: name@bwalletx.com.
  • Your friends can come too. Bring your HandCash friends into your bWalletX address book.

Your bWalletX keys are your own, made on your device, as with any other account.

Your address book, in and out

You can import an address book and export yours. It’s your list, and you can take it with you. This is in bWalletX 5.2.25.

Invites with tokens

A host picks people, from the people they follow on X, their HandCash friends or a pasted list, and sends each one an invite with some of the room’s token attached. Invites arrive as airdrops in bMail. The token is the ticket into the Space.

The person invited opens the link, signs in with X or HandCash, and gets bWalletX. The token arrives in their wallet and they’re let into the room’s chat straight away. As their contacts join, they’re matched to paymails and show up as friends.

Keep it safe

  • The link carries the token, so don’t forward it.
  • Room entry is tied to the invited X account.
  • We never hold the tokens. They go from the host’s wallet to yours.

More on the Invites page.

The first room: The Scammer Cartel

The first room to use invites is The Scammer Cartel. Its people will come in through invites, straight into the room’s chat and its Space: bchatx.com/s/scammerscartel.
