bMusicX: every second pays the artist

bMusicX, the Bitcoin Music Exchange, streams music by the second, and every second pays the artist directly.

Albums on a shelf

Swipe through covers, then open an album shelf with play buttons. New this week: the WELCOME TO TERRORNODE soundtrack, its Alternate Takes, the Void Mass EP and Paper Reed Circuit (Part B). Every album has samples to try.

Paid by the second

Listening pays the artist as you go, straight to their wallet. On VexVoid’s Echoes in the Abyss, the first track is free and the next five are sponsored by VexVoid for bWalletX listeners.

Fans who pay count

Artists are ranked by what listeners actually paid them over 30 days. Self-payments don’t count.
