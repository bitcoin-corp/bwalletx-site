Friends, bPics and video in rooms

Friends has its own page on bWalletX Desktop, bPics looks like a photo app, and video links play inside the Feed and chat rooms.

Friends

On Desktop, Wallet → Friends shows your friends as a grid with their gold bAvatars and handles. Search, sort and star favourites. Add someone by $handle, paymail, @handle or QR code; you see who it is before you add them, and lookalike names are flagged.

Open a friend to Send, Request, Message, Invite to a Space or Send an NFT, see what you’ve sent each other, and keep a private nickname and notes. Each friend has their own link.

bPics

bPics now looks like a photo app: a tidy grid of your pictures, a full-screen view with the details, and drag and drop to mint a new one.

Video in the Feed and rooms

YouTube, Vimeo and video file links now play inside the Feed and chat rooms. Nothing loads until you tap play. In rooms you can also share photos and videos (up to 25 MB) with the paperclip.
