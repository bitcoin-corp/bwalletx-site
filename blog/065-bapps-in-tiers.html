The bApps screen

bApps are now laid out in tiers: our own b…X apps first, then partner apps, then community apps that meet the bApp standard.

Easy to find

Our own apps come first, then partner apps such as VexVoid and Zero Dice. Tiles sit two to a row in colour, and in the Chrome extension a bApp opens in its own tab.

The bApp standard

A community app joins the main list when it has a token, a bChatX room, a wallet theme and payments inside the wallet. Others fold away under Not yet bApp-ready, so the list stays useful.
