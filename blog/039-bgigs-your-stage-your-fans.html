bGigs: your stage, your fans

Artists can play live to their fans from anywhere, sell tickets at the door or by the minute, and get paid straight away. It works today in bWalletX.

Your own stage

Every artist gets a Space with a link that never changes. Go live on camera and mic from your phone or the web, bring a guest up on stage, and talk to the room while you play.

It works across the world: the promoter can run the room in London while the artist plays from Tokyo.

Tickets that pay you

Set a door price and fans pay once to come in. Or charge by the minute, so fans pay only for what they hear and can leave whenever they like.

Either way the money goes straight to the artist, as it comes in. No waiting weeks for a payout.

What's not done

  • Playing your music video into the room, in sync for everyone, on repeat if you like.
  • Album Spaces: a ticketed run of your music videos, one after another.
  • Splitting ticket money between promoter, label, artist and crew.
  • Tickets are in bWalletX (web, Android and Chrome), not the App Store or Google Play editions.
