Development blog

How bWallet and bWalletX were built, one chapter at a time. Every post is written from the git history of the wallet, this site, the chat server and the PNEEs prototype. It covers what we built, why, what broke, what we reversed and what is still not done.

Newest first. Dates come from commits. bWallet is a fork of Yours Wallet (MIT licence).
