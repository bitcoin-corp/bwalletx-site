The Exchange, sorted

The Market in bWalletX Desktop is now the Exchange. Browse by category, platform and tag, swap in place, and find your friends: buy a little of someone’s own token and open their room in one go.

The Market is now the Exchange

On Desktop, /exchange shows every market with its price, 24-hour change, volume and how many are for sale. Star the ones you care about. Open one for its chart, every live listing, and Buy or Sell, with the fees in BSV and dollars before you confirm. Swap and My orders are tabs on the same page.

Sorted by category, platform and tag

Tap a category such as Games or Social, pick the platform a token comes from, or search by tag. Each choice is in the link, so you can share exactly what you’re looking at. Token pages show their category, platform and tags too.

Friends

The Friends filter lists your friends’ own tokens first, then other people on bWalletX. Buy & chat buys the cheapest one on offer in a single step and opens that person’s room. If nobody is selling yet, it says so and lets you open their room.

Your friend page shows their token too. New personal tokens start with a supply of 1 billion.

Related: Exchange · Friends · Swap into BSV

Update, 30 Oct 2026: the Friends filter has since been removed from the Exchange. Your friends’ own tokens are on the Friends page.
