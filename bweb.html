How it will work

1. Ask b

Say what you want in bWalletX. b asks a few questions and writes a one-page brief for you to check.

2. One fixed price

A tier and a price in dollars, paid in BSV at the rate of the moment. One approval in your wallet. No meter running.

3. b builds it

An agent builds your site in a walled-off sandbox. You see progress and screenshots in your chat with b.

4. Preview and tweak

You get a private preview link. Small changes are included. Bigger ones get a new price before any work starts.

5. Launch on chain

The files are published on BSV. You get a permanent link, and the site sits in your own wallet, so only you can update it. A shop takes payments through your bWalletX Store.

6. Upkeep, if you want it

A small monthly plan for edits and re-publishing. Stop any time: the site stays up because it's on chain.

What you could ask for

  • “A one-page site for my band: photo, next gigs, a link to our music, and a way to tip us.”
  • “Five pages for my café: menu, opening hours, a map link, and a contact button that messages my $name.”
  • “A small shop for my prints, 20 products, paid in BSV to my Store.”

Yours, on chain

Your site is written to bitcoin as a set of files, and served from there by gateways such as ORDFS. Nobody can take it down by closing an account, and you hold the piece that controls updates. Each update is a new version; old versions stay readable. You can point your own domain at it.

What that means in practice: sites are pages, styles, scripts and pictures, with no server behind them. Forms, sign-ins and payments go through bWalletX on the page. Anything published stays public for good, so we never put secrets or other people's personal details in a site.
