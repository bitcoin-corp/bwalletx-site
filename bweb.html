bWeb: your web on bitcoin Coming

bWeb is a plan, not a product yet. Nothing on this page is built. Here is the idea: you start in bWalletX, and your bApps become yours. You choose how they look and what they show, and when you want more, agents like b build it for you. You pay in small amounts, from your wallet, only after you approve.

Three ways to make it yours

1. Make it yours Free

Settings for each app: the order of your bar, the theme, what the Feed shows, how much a Like costs and your daily cap. Instant. Most people never need more.

2. Ask b Small paid job

"b, add a word-count goal to bWriter." b shows you a price and what the add-on can and can't do. You approve in your wallet, and it's live in minutes. Undo any time.

3. Your own version Paid

b makes your own copy of an open-source bApp with the change you asked for, on its own web address. Good changes are offered back to the main app, so everyone gets them.

Safety promises

  • Add-ons never touch your keys. They run walled off from your wallet and can't send your data anywhere.
  • Payments only go through your wallet. bWalletX shows its own approval and your daily cap applies. Nothing pays on its own.
  • Your settings are private. They're encrypted to you, and the apps never see them.

bApp tokens are keys

Each bApp's token (such as $bWriter) is a key to that app's rooms, including its build room, where people who hold it talk about and rank what to build next. If you paid for a change that goes into the main app, you get a record that you helped build it. A token is room access, not an investment, and it doesn't pay you anything.

Example: a movie with your friends

In your group's room: "b, a heist movie starring me, Sam and Priya, in Lisbon." b gives a price. Everyone in the cast signs their own consent in their own wallet; without everyone's yes, nothing gets made. The group funds it together, and the film is delivered privately to the room. Posting it to the public bMovies feed needs every cast member to agree again.
