Coordinate on BSV. Talk where the money is.

BSV is a messaging protocol at heart. Every transaction can carry a message. So the conversation about BSV should live on BSV, in the same wallet that holds your coins and tokens.

Get bWalletXOpen bChatX

Why

Twitter, Reddit and Telegram put every community in one room, and BSV threads there get buried or piled on. We still post there, because that is where new people are. But the planning should happen on BSV, where every account is a wallet and, as paid messages arrive, spam costs money. Advertise there, coordinate here. The full argument: 021 Coordinate on BSV.

Chat in your wallet

Chat is part of bWalletX. You don’t sign up anywhere else: your wallet is your account, and the people you talk to can pay you, and you them, in the same place. bChatX is the full version, on the web and inside the wallet.

Other BSV networks are already here, and Chat reads from them rather than replacing them:

Twetch

One of the longest-running BSV social apps.

Treechat

Threaded posts and replies on BSV.

Fwetch

Boards on the Vulpine network.

Peck

A BSV feed built on Bitcoin Schema.

bChatX

Our full chat app. Rooms, feed and messages.

Twetch, Treechat, Fwetch and Peck are made by other teams, not The Bitcoin Corporation.

What Chat does

Each item is marked by what the code does today.

  • LiveMessage boards. Open rooms in bChat: public or invite-only, anyone signed in can start one. The bChat feed also reads Twetch, Treechat, Fwetch and Peck posts in one place.
  • LiveToken-gated rooms. Hold a BSV-21 token or 1Sat collection and its room appears in your Chat list. Sell it and the room goes. bChat checks the holding on every read and post. Not in bWallet, the App Store and Google Play edition.
  • LivePrivate messages. Direct messages between wallets, with blocking and reporting.
  • LiveVoice and video calls. One-to-one calls between friends. Either side can turn the camera on or off during a call.
  • LiveTips. Tip the author of a post in BSV, paid to their own verified address.
  • LiveBroadcast into a Space. The host or a speaker shares a tab or screen, with its sound, to everyone in the Space (since 5.2.17). Not in the store edition.
  • Live since 5.1.85Pay per message. In token rooms the wallet now charges the room’s price per message: the message and the payment go in one transaction (50-sat minimum).
  • Partly livePay per second. Paid calls by the second are live in bPhone (5.1.87). Paying for a stream by the second is next.
  • Partly livePay with tokens. bPhone calls can already be paid in MNEE or any BSV-21 token (5.1.87). Paying for messages in a token is next.
  • Partly liveSet your price to be reached. You can already charge to receive calls with bPhone (5.1.87). A price for strangers to message you, with friends free, is next.
