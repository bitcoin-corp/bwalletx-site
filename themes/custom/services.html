03 / 04  Good to know

Good to know.

  • The share register mirror isn’t your legal register. Your company keeps its register of members as the law requires. If the two differ, the register wins.
  • The deal record is a record-keeping tool. It isn’t legal or compliance advice and doesn’t make your business compliant on its own. Your compliance duties stay yours.
  • We never hold your money, your users’ money, art or keys. Every wallet is self-custody.
  • We don’t issue, sell or promote shares or tokens for you, and we don’t arrange your deals.
  • No calls or response-time promises below a custom build. Support is in your room and by email.
  • Cancel any monthly service on 30 days’ notice.
