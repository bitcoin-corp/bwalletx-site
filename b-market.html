A store page you already know

Every shop gets a page at bmarketx.com/str/your-shop, laid out the way eBay buyers and sellers are used to: your banner, your feedback, your listings in a grid, prices in US dollars. Nobody has to learn a new shop.

Move from eBay in about a minute

Two ways in. Pick whichever is easier.

Upload your listings file

In eBay Seller Hub, download the “All active listings” CSV and upload it on bmarketx.com/sell. Your listings come across in one go.

Or paste your store link

Paste your eBay store link, then put the short code we give you into your eBay store description. That proves the store is yours, and it brings over your feedback score, items sold and followers too.

Your wallet is your account

Sign in with bWalletX. There is no password to choose, forget or leak. bMarketX uses the open BRC-100 wallet standard.

Mint your whole shop, if you like

  1. Each listing becomes a BSV-21 token in your own bWalletX.
  2. Its photo is inscribed on chain as the token’s icon.
  3. The supply is your stock: ten teapots, ten tokens.

About a tenth of a cent a listing

Minting costs about $0.001 per listing in network fees, so a 682-item store is around 75 cents. The wallet shows the exact price before you confirm. The tokens are yours, held in your wallet, not by bMarketX.

Dollars on the tag, Bitcoin at the till

Prices show in US dollars. Buyers will pay in BSV from bWalletX.

What isn’t live yet

Buying opens soon. Checkout and Buy It Now, shipping, and Sign in with eBay are still being built, so nothing can be bought on bMarketX today. Moving your shop and minting it work now, so your shop is ready the day buying opens.

Questions

Do I have to leave eBay?

No. Bringing your shop over copies it; your eBay store keeps running as it is.

Is bMarketX.com part of eBay?

No. bMarketX is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay. We name eBay because that’s where most sellers are coming from.

Who owns my minted listings?

You do. They sit in your own wallet, and your keys stay on your device. We can’t move them.

Do I need to mint?

No. Minting is optional. Your store page works either way.
