Cards you make with b

Tell b who it’s for and what they love. b makes the card. You mint it as your own NFT, send it by bMail, and they keep it in their wallet or wear it as their bAvatar on bChatX.

How it works

1. Make it with b

Describe the card in a sentence. b generates the picture with AI image generation and shows you a few to choose from.

2. Mint it

The card goes on Bitcoin SV as an NFT in your name, from the Mint in bWalletX. One tap, no files to juggle.

3. Send it by bMail

Pick a $name and the card arrives in their bMail, ready to open.

4. Keep it or wear it

It sits in their wallet as theirs. If they love it, they set it as their round bAvatar on bChatX and in their wallet.

What it costs

You pay in BSV, from your wallet, and you see the total before you confirm. It has three parts:

  • The generation. Making a picture with AI costs us real money, so you pay for it, plus a small premium on top.
  • The minting fee. The Bitcoin SV network fee to put the card on chain, plus the usual bWalletX fee. See Mint with bWalletX for how minting is priced.
  • The stamp. Sending by bMail works like the penny post.

We’ll publish the exact prices when card making goes live. Nothing is charged without your OK on the wallet’s pay sheet.

Bigger than birthdays

Cards are the first thing we’re building on Generation-as-a-Service: we run the AI on our servers, you pay per generation in BSV, and what you make is yours to mint. Thank-you cards, invitations, wedding cards and more kinds of generation will follow for everyone with bWalletX.
