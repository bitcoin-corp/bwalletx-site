Launchpad. Launch your own coin, with something behind it.

The Launchpad lists bonding-curve coins made on TokenBlaster. You can buy and sell them in bWalletX, and launch your own from the wallet.

Buy and sell in the wallet

Live since 5.1.74 Open Exchange and tap the Launchpad bar. The wallet checks every trade before you sign it.

Launch your own coin

Live since 5.1.85 A Launch your own coin button at the top of the Launchpad opens TokenBlaster’s launch page inside the wallet, with your wallet connected. On the web you can also launch at tokenblaster.lol/launch/new.

Launch it with something

A coin should come with something people can use: a website, an app or a product, and somewhere to talk, such as X, Telegram or a bChat room. Links are encouraged, not required. The plan is that coins with them rank higher on the board.

Live since 5.1.85 Token pages in the wallet show those links as chips. Tapping one warns you first: it is an outside site, not checked by us, and you should never enter your recovery phrase there.

What a coin gives you

By default, one thing: holders can enter the coin’s bChat room. Anything else is the issuer’s business. If the issuer describes other uses, the wallet shows them as “Issuer says: …”, marked as not checked by bWalletX.

Creator vesting and airdrops

  • ComingCreator vesting. Creators can keep 0 to 20% (default 10%), always time-locked: by default a 12-month cliff, then 24 months of linear unlocking, never less than 6 months, with a public receipt. See Token locking.
  • ComingAirdrops to holders. The way to reward holders is to send them tokens. Holders can already receive and keep airdrops in the inbox (5.1.85); a send-to-all-holders tool for issuers is next. See Airdrops.

No promises of returns

Live since 5.1.85 When you create a token in bWalletX and its description promises returns, profits, dividends or similar, the wallet warns you while you type and won’t sign until the wording is gone: “Remove wording that promises returns before publishing. Describe airdrops or room access instead.” It is a word filter, not legal advice.

Not in the store edition

bWallet, the App Store and Google Play edition, has no Launchpad.
