Verify a signed note

Notes and IOUs signed in bWalletX (Settings › Sign a note) carry a signature from the signer's identity key. Paste the note file or open it here to see who signed it, when, and that not a word has changed. It's checked in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

A valid signature proves the holder of that identity key signed this exact text. To know whose key it is, check it against their bWalletX profile or paymail. bWalletX
