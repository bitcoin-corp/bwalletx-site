Play with bWalletX

The games ask a BRC-100 wallet for permission, the same way any app on Bitcoin SV does. bWalletX is the wallet tokenblaster.lol is built around.

  1. Open the game

    On your phone: in bWalletX, open the Apps tab and tap Games. Games open in the wallet’s own browser, so the wallet is already there. On a computer: visit tokenblaster.lol with the bWalletX extension installed, or tap Connect and scan the QR code with bWalletX on your phone.

  2. Load your gun

    Choose LIVE and approve one load of 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 sats. That is the only approval: the game then plays from those sats without asking again. Unload any time to send what’s left back to your wallet.

  3. Play

    In LIVE mode each shot, jump or card is one transaction: 1 sat to The Bitcoin Corporation plus the network fee (about 26 sats). In Arena you can fire tokens from your wallet instead; hit another player and that token lands in their gun. Your best runs go on the leaderboards.

Nothing here is a bet: games don’t pay out prizes. Coin Pop and Token Potions cost 10p a game, or you can practise free. Play with small amounts.
