The agent wallet

Give your AI agent a trading account and a strategy, and it trades for you: buying, selling, sending and minting, inside the budget you set. Stop it or sweep everything back any time.

Fund a trading account→ Load a strategy→ Your agent trades

Agent accounts

An agent account is a separate account with its own keys. You decide how much goes in; that balance is all an agent can ever spend. Your other accounts are never touched.

Fund it

Move BSV or tokens in from any of your accounts, in one tap.

Stop it

Stop one agent account, or Stop all agents at once. Stopped accounts refuse every agent action until you resume them.

Sweep back

Move every token, NFT and all the BSV back to another account in one go.

See everything

An activity log lists every action an agent took, with links to the transactions. Add an optional daily spending cap and labels like "long-term" or "$HANDLE fund".

Many agents

Run one agent per account, or one agent across several. A Trader on two strategy accounts, a Treasurer that tops them up, a Reporter that only reads: each sees only the accounts you give it.

Strategies

Agree a strategy with your agent in plain words, and it follows that strategy and nothing else. Make your own in chat, or buy one. How strategies work →
