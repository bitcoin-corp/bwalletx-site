Ask b

Ask b is the AI assistant built into bWalletX. Press and hold the big b in the middle of the bottom bar to talk to it (a tap goes home): it knows how the wallet works, helps you design strategies, and in chat rooms you can call it in with /b.

Looking for agent accounts: AIs with their own wallet?

Separate accounts that an AI runs for you, each with its own budget and its own ghost. Agent accounts →

Two ways to use it

Your own key

Add an API key for Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter. It stays in secure storage on your device and b’s requests go straight from your phone to that provider, which handles them under its own terms. Anything you report to b still comes to our team.

Pay per message

The wallet gets a quote, pays in BSV and gets the answer, with a daily limit. The price is set in US dollars and converted to sats at quote time. Pay per message is in bWalletX only.

In chats: /b

Type /b followed by your question in a chat room and Ask b answers there, paid in BSV.

What it can do

Explain a feature Create a strategy Trade with a strategy Buy or sell one

It has the wallet's usage guide as its system prompt, so it can explain features. In an agent account it can act, buying and sending, but only through the strategy rules the wallet enforces, never on its own judgement. How strategies work →

Privacy and consent

Talking to b means talking to us. b is our team’s assistant, not a private chat, and it is not end-to-end encrypted. We read and keep conversations with b for up to 180 days, with secrets stripped, to fix bugs and improve the app. How we handle chats with b →

Before the first message to each provider, a consent sheet shows what is sent (your message and the last 8 turns) and what isn't. You can revoke consent in Settings. The wallet refuses to send anything that looks like a recovery phrase or private key. Each reply only sees the recent conversation, but our team keeps the conversations as described above.
