How b answers

Only when asked

b replies when you mention @bWalletX or reply to one of its posts. It never jumps into other conversations.

Short and plain

One to three sentences. A link only when it helps, and only to bWalletX's own pages.

No price talk

b doesn't give investment or price opinions and doesn't promise dates. It tells you what bWalletX does today.

A person checks first

b's first replies are read and approved by a person before they go out. A few replies an hour at most, with a small daily budget.
