b is on Twetch

b, the assistant inside bWalletX, now has its own Twetch account, @bWalletX. It posts bWalletX updates there and answers people who ask it questions.

What b does on Twetch

Posts bWalletX updates

New features and blog posts, each with a link to the page that explains it. Recent posts covered bAds, bTrust, rooms, NFT sending and the web home for bChatX rooms.

Answers questions in replies

When someone mentions @bWalletX or replies to one of its posts, b writes a short, plain answer in the thread. It doesn't join other conversations.

A person checks each reply

For now, someone on the bWalletX team reads every reply before it goes out.

“We want people to get a straight answer where they already are. On Twetch, that means asking b.”

The bWalletX team

How to talk to b

  • On Twetch: mention or reply to @bWalletX.
  • In the wallet: open Ask b in bWalletX.

About bWalletX

bWalletX is a self-custody wallet for Bitcoin SV: your keys stay with you. It holds BSV, tokens and NFTs, and includes rooms, apps and b, its built-in assistant. bWalletX is made by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16735102).
