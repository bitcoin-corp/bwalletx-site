Invites. The token is the ticket.

Bring your people into a Space. Each invite carries a small amount of the room’s token, and holding it is what lets them in.

Get bWalletXAbout Spaces

For the host

  • Pick peopleChoose from the people you follow on X, your HandCash friends, or paste a list of names.
  • Attach a tokenEach invite carries some of the room’s token. That token is their ticket into the Space.
  • SendInvites arrive as airdrops in bMail, like any other airdrop.

For the person invited

  • Open the linkTap the invite link.
  • Sign inSign in with X, or with HandCash, and get bWalletX.
  • The token arrivesIt lands in your new wallet.
  • You’re inYou’re let into the room’s chat straight away.

Friends find each other

As the people in your contacts join, they’re matched to their paymails and show up as friends. You don’t have to look anyone up.

Keep it safe

  • The link carries the token. Don’t forward it. Whoever opens it first can claim it.
  • Room entry is tied to the invited X account. Someone else holding the link can’t walk into the room as you.
  • We never hold the tokens. They go from the host’s wallet to yours.
