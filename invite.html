Invites. The token is the ticket.

Bring your people into a Space. Each invite carries a small amount of the room’s token, and holding it is what lets them in.

Get bWalletXAbout Spaces

There are two kinds of invite. They do different jobs.

Open Drops

A link holding some of a token. The first person to open it claims it. Good for a post or a group chat, where you don’t know exactly who will come.

  • Make a dropPut some of the room’s token into a link.
  • Share itPost it anywhere.
  • First to open claims itThey sign in with X or HandCash, get bWalletX, and the token lands in their wallet.

Named Invites

An invite for one person. Only the X account you name can claim it, and the token is sent to them automatically when they do.

  • Pick peopleChoose X accounts, from the people you follow, your HandCash friends or a pasted list.
  • Attach a tokenEach invite carries some of the room’s token. That token is their ticket into the Space.
  • They sign inThe named person signs in with that X account, gets bWalletX, and the token arrives. They’re let into the room’s chat.

Friends find each other

Planned: as the people in your contacts join, they’ll be matched to their paymails and show up as friends. You won’t have to look anyone up.

Keep it safe

  • An Open Drop link carries the token. Whoever opens it first claims it, so share it only where you mean to.
  • A Named Invite works only for the X account it names. Someone else holding the link can’t claim it.
  • We never hold the tokens. They go from the host’s wallet to yours.
