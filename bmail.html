How it works

1 · WRITE

Write to a $handle

Address your letter to anyone’s $handle, the same name they use for payments and chat.

2 · STAMP

Add a stamp

1¢ Penny post, 3¢ Priority, or Reply paid so they can answer you for free. You see the full price before anything is sent.

3 · DELIVERED

It arrives sealed

Only they can read it. The biggest stamps rise to the top of their inbox. Friends are always free and always on top.

Why stamps

  • Stamps stop spam. Sending a million letters costs real money, so nobody does it. Sending one costs a penny.
  • You get paid for your attention. The stamp goes to you, the person reading, not to us.
  • Requests holds the rest. Unstamped mail from strangers and token airdrops wait quietly in Requests, without notifications. Look when you like: some of them turn out to matter.

Your price, your rules. Everyone starts at the Penny post, 1¢. You can raise your price to reach you, and mail below it goes to Requests.

Questions

Who gets the stamp?

The person you write to. Payment goes wallet to wallet, and bMail takes no cut.

Do I pay to write to friends?

No. Friends are free and always on top of your inbox.

When can I use it?

Now. bMail is in bWalletX: tap the mailbox icon at the top of the app.
