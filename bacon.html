Six degrees

bChatX shows profiles syndicated from X at bchatx.com/x/<handle>. When you claim yours by signing in with X, you can bring 7 friends free. Everyone is only a few friends away.

$BACON

$BACON is a BSV-21 memecoin with a supply of 1,000,000,000. You get $BACON for each friend you bring who claims their profile. It is a collectible and a thank-you, nothing more.

Bacon Notes

Bacon Notes are one-of-a-kind, banknote-style collectibles. You get one for every 6 friends who claim their profile.

  • Rasher guilloche and filigree, a pig portrait and a serial number on every note.
  • Face values of 1K, 10K, 100K and 1M $BACON.
  • Rare golden notes and misprints.
  • Each note holds its $BACON, just like a PoWNote.
