Six degrees

bChatX shows profiles syndicated from X at bchatx.com/x/<handle>. When you claim yours by signing in with X, you can bring 7 friends free. Everyone is only a few friends away.

$BACON

$BACON is a BSV-21 memecoin with a supply of 1,000,000,000. You get $BACON for each friend you bring who claims their profile. It is a collectible and a thank-you, nothing more.

Bacon Notes

Bacon Notes are numbered, banknote-style collectibles. You get one for every friend who joins bChatX because of you, and one more each time one of their friends joins.

  • A propaganda-print banknote on streaky bacon, with the Leader, a boar, in a cap, beret, ushanka or top hat.
  • Mined on your own device: the proof-of-work hash seeds the art.
  • Golden notes 1 in 50, misprints 1 in 100. Every note is the same kind of note; only the number and the art differ.
  • Minting coming soon. See Bacon Notes.
