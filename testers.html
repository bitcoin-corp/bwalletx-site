How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and apply. Use the Google account on your Android phone.
  2. I read every application and approve testers by hand. If you're in, I add your email to the Google Play test and you'll see your tester code here.
  3. Join the test on Google Play and install bWallet from there (not the APK from this site).
  4. Open bWallet, go to Settings › Testing and enter your tester code. The app checks in once a day when you open it.
  5. Open bWallet every day for 14 days. Use it like you normally would: send a little, receive, try the features.

What you get

  • 0.25 BSV after you first open the app installed from Google Play.
  • 0.75 BSV once you've checked in on at least 12 of the 14 days.
  • Paid to your own bWallet receive address. I pay each one by hand and you'll see the transaction id here.

The rules

  • One place per person: one Google account, one phone, one wallet. Duplicates get declined.
  • Payment never depends on ratings or reviews. Please don't leave a review because of this; Google doesn't allow paid reviews. Report bugs and ideas in the app instead (Settings › Contact and reports).
  • I approve applicants, and I can say no or remove a place if something looks wrong.
  • 20 places. When they're gone, they're gone.
  • What the app sends while you test is in the privacy policy: a random install id, your tester code, whether it came from Google Play, and the app version. Nothing about your keys or money.
