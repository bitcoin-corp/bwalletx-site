How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and apply. Use the Google account on your Android phone.
  2. I read every application and approve testers by hand. If you're in, I add your email to the Google Play test and you'll see your tester code here.
  3. Join the test on Google Play and install bWallet from there (not the APK from this site).
  4. Open bWallet, go to Settings › Testing and enter your tester code. The app checks in once a day when you open it.
  5. Open bWallet every day for 14 days. Use it like you normally would: send a little, receive, try the features.

What you get

  • 0.25 BSV after you first open the app installed from Google Play.
  • 0.75 BSV once you've checked in on at least 12 of the 14 days.
  • Paid from our tester pot to your own bWallet receive address. Every payment is listed below with its transaction.
