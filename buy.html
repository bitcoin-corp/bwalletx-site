How it works

Tap Buy BSV

The gold card at the top of your wallet opens the Get BSV sheet.

Pick how

Pay with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank. Or receive from an exchange, another wallet or a friend.

It lands in your wallet

Your BSV shows up in your wallet, in dollars. Ready to send, mint or trade.

Ways to get BSV

From an exchange or another wallet

bWalletX shows your BSV address and QR code. Send from wherever your BSV is now.

From a friend

Share your $name or paymail. They send to it and it arrives in seconds.

With a card or bank

Pick a provider in the sheet. It opens in the wallet’s browser. Copy your address from Receive and paste it there.

The card and bank providers in the sheet today:

  • Ramp · Apple Pay, Google Pay, card or bank
  • BSV Association · buy page run by the BSV Association
  • Guardarian · card or bank transfer
  • ChangeNOW · card, many currencies
  • Alchemy Pay · card, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • Onramper · compares several card providers
  • cex.io · card; needs an exchange account

Good to know

These providers are independent companies, not bWalletX. They check your ID and set their own fees and limits, and what’s on offer depends on where you live. bWalletX never holds your money: you pay the provider, and the BSV goes straight to your own wallet. We’re working on buying with Ramp fully inside the app, with your address filled in for you. It isn’t live yet.
