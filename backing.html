Reading this page

The ratio is the vault’s BSV value divided by its PNEEs. A vault can mint up to 1000%. Below 150% (shown in red), anyone may close it by burning its PNEEs and taking BSV worth them plus 10%; the rest goes back to its owner. Vaults under 200% are shown in gold. The price here is the median of public sources right now; a vault’s own last price round is the one it last used on-chain. All five price signers are run by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd; how PNEEs work.

Data: bwallet.space/api/pnee?op=vaults, read from BSV.
