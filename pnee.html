PNEEs · pennies backed by BSV

A PNEE is one US cent on BSV. Every PNEE was minted against BSV locked in a vault, and every vault is public. You can lock your own BSV, mint PNEEs against it, spend them, and close the vault by burning them.

How it works

Lock

In bWalletX, Wallet › PNEEs › the lock icon. You choose how much BSV to lock. It goes into a vault script on BSV that is owned by a key of your wallet. Nobody else can move it while the vault is above its floor.

Mint

You can mint PNEEs up to a tenth of the vault’s value: $10 of BSV backs up to $1 of PNEEs (a ratio of 1000%). The script checks this itself against a fresh BSV price, so a mint that goes over the limit is not a valid transaction. You can mint in steps until the vault reaches 10x.

Spend

PNEEs move like any token: send some to another bWalletX user and the rest comes back to you. A send can’t create or lose PNEEs: the script makes the amounts add up.

Close

To unlock your BSV, burn exactly the PNEEs the vault minted. The close transaction burns them and sends all the vault’s BSV back to your wallet, less the network fee. There is no fee for the vault itself.

Top up

You can add BSV to a vault at any time. It raises the ratio and the amount the vault can back.

The safety valve: the 150% floor

If the BSV price falls until a vault holds less than 150% of the value of its PNEEs, anyone may close that vault. They burn all its PNEEs and take BSV worth those PNEEs plus 10%. The rest of the vault’s BSV goes back to its owner, paid by the script. From 1000%, the price has to fall by 85% before a vault reaches the floor. The backing page shows which vaults are near it.

Who runs the price signers

The BSV price a vault uses is signed by five price signers; a mint or a close below the floor needs three of them on the same round, and the script takes their median. Today all five signer keys are run by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd on the bwallet.space server, from public price sources. The same server holds the burn attester key: it co-signs a close only after checking that the PNEEs being burned were minted by a vault and add up to the vault’s PNEEs. Both keys are fixed into each vault when it is opened, and their public values are at bwallet.space/api/pnee?op=config.

The contract

The vault and the token are two sCrypt contracts, compiled to Bitcoin Script. Read them here: bondVault.ts and bondToken.ts. The wallet builds each vault from the compiled template whose fingerprint is listed in README.txt, and checks every script byte for byte against test transactions.

Earlier pilot

Before vaults were in the wallet, a capped pilot ran on mainnet with vaults opened from the command line. Its first close below the floor was a labelled demo vault: 7d496a59…, where the script paid the closer the PNEEs plus 10% and returned 890,493 sats to the owner.
