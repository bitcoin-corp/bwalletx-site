CoinGeek on b

CoinGeek writes about Bitcoin SV news. In bWalletX, its headlines sit in bNews next to other tech news, and every story gets its own thread where readers talk on chain.

What you can do

Headlines in bNews

Open Feed and switch to bNews. CoinGeek is one of its sources. We show the headline, picture, time and link only, never the article itself: tap through to read it on coingeek.com.

A thread for every story

Each story opens as a thread. Posts there are ordinary on-chain bChat posts that link the story, so the discussion is on chain and nobody’s server owns it.

Tip the people in the thread

Tip, like or lock behind the posts other readers write about a story, the same as anywhere in the Feed.
