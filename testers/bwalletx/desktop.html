Test bWalletX Desktop.

bWalletX is the full edition: wallet, Exchange, names, chat, calls and apps. This page says how to join, exactly what to test, and how to tell us what you find.

On what

DeviceMac, Windows, Linux or ChromeOS
SystemChrome or Edge, a wide window
Viadesktop.bwalletx.com (installable)
EditionbWalletX Desktop

Other tests: Chrome extension · web · Desktop · bWallet on iPhone and Android.

Join

  1. Open desktop.bwalletx.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Install it as an app from the install icon in the address bar, so it gets its own window.
  3. Create a wallet or restore from your 12 words.

What to test, in this order

  1. First run.Create a wallet, write down the 12 words, set a password. Then restore it from the words. Does everything come back?
  2. Receive and send.Send yourself a few pence of BSV, then pay a $handle and a plain address. Does the fee shown match what left?
  3. Names.Search a name. Get a free one, sell one you hold, give one to a friend.
  4. Exchange.Use the gold filter button, open a token, look at the Launchpad and Names tabs.
  5. Chat and calls.Send a message in a room and a DM; call another user.
  6. Tell b.Open Ask b and report anything odd. It is logged for the team with a reference, even without an AI key.
  7. Wide layout.Resize the window from narrow to full screen. Does every page use the space, with nothing cut off?

What to tell us

Anything slow, stuck, unclear, or that cost more than you expected: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and your browser and device.

Two ways to send it

Tell b in the wallet, or email support@bwalletx.com. Keep small amounts in a test wallet and never send anyone your 12 words.
