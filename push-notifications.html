Push notifications

Never miss a message. Push notifications are live since version 5.1.70 and reach you even with the app closed: on your phone, the web wallet and the Chrome extension. They run through our own push server, push.bwalletx.com.

LiveiPhoneAndroidWebChrome extension

Where it works

iPhone

Apple Push Notifications.

Android

Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Web wallet and Chrome extension

Web Push, so your browser can tell you even when the tab isn't open.

Turning it on

Go to Settings › Notifications › Push notifications. In the phone apps the switch starts on. In the web wallet and the Chrome extension it starts off: turn it on there and your browser asks for permission. The switch is per device; previews and quiet hours belong to your account and follow you to every device.

What notifies you

  • Direct messages from friends, always.
  • Group chats: each group has a bell with All, Mentions or Off. Groups start on Mentions, so busy rooms don't buzz you all day.
  • Mentions of your $name.
  • Photos, voice and video messages sent to a room you're in.
  • $b answers to your /b questions.
  • Requests to sign or witness a document.

Tapping a notification opens the room or screen it's about. Several messages in quick succession are collapsed into one.

Privacy

By default a notification says who messaged you, not what they wrote, so nothing private shows on your lock screen. If you want message previews, turn on Show message previews. Quiet hours silence notifications overnight or whenever you choose. No message text is stored on our push server.

You can turn notifications off per group with the bell, or entirely in Settings › Notifications. Your keys never leave your device; notifications don't need them.

bWalletX and bWallet

Notifications work the same in both editions: bWalletX here, and bWallet, the App Store and Google Play version.
