Handles. Your name is your paymail.

There is no account ID and no email needed: your 12 words are the account. On top of them you get a $handle people can pay and message, and it can be your token and your room too.

Get bWalletX

Need to prove who you are in the real world, privately? See Identity.

$handles

  • Live$handles. A name people can pay and message, which is also your paymail.
  • LiveNumbered handles. New accounts get a free numbered handle, from $bwallet0101 onward. $bwallet0001 to 0100 are reserved.
  • LiveRenames. Change to a name you prove you hold. Your rooms and messages move with you.
  • LiveYour token and your room. In bWalletX your $handle can be a personal token, with a room named after you. See Features.
  • ComingMore than one handle per account. The groundwork is in; adding a second handle is not.
  • ComingHandle NFTs. Own your handle as an NFT you can hold or sell, separate from your account.

Linked accounts ($401)

Link other accounts to your on-chain identity. Each link adds to your identity strength, so others can see more than one source vouches for you.

  • LiveIn the wallet: X and Google, under Settings › Identity.
  • LiveOn bit-sign: X, Google, GitHub, Discord and LinkedIn.

Card signature

  • LiveYour signature on your card. Draw it once. It shows on the back of your wallet card. It stays on your device and is not put on chain.

Signing documents and contracts is on /sign.

Sign in

  • LiveSign in with bWalletX. Apps can let you sign in with your wallet instead of a password. Your wallet signs a one-time challenge; no password or email is shared. For developers: /connect.
  • LiveSign in with X. Get a verified name from your X account, with your photo: /x.
