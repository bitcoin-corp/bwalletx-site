Kintsugi (金継ぎ): mending broken pottery with gold, so the repair shows.

b Kintsugi: help us build b

Our program for building b: bWalletX, bChatX and the bApps. It works both ways. We pay developers to build b, wallet to wallet: each job is a microcontract with a fixed price, and you get the stated price in full. And if you want a feature or a fix, you can pay us to build it. Developers who build with Claude verify their ID, pass one entry task and take the work. For jobs between users, there is bJobsX.
