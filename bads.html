Our ads aren’t ads. They’re bAds. A bAd is made by b, the agent that lives in your bWalletX. You tell b about your business in a sentence. b makes the film, the logo or the whole brand, in your colours, not ours. Say “b ad” out loud and you get the joke. We’re leaning in: b Bad.
