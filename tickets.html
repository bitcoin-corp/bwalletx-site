For fans

  1. It lands in your walletBuy or RSVP, and the ticket goes straight into bWalletX with the event, your tier and the doors time.
  2. One all-in priceWhat you see is what you pay. No fees added at the end.
  3. A code that changes at the doorYour wallet signs a fresh code every few seconds. Staff scan it, and a copy is no use to anyone.
  4. Pass it on at face valueCan’t make it? Pass your ticket to someone else through bTicketsX, at face value.
  5. Keep the stubAfter the show the ticket stays in your wallet as a stub: proof you were there.

For organisers

  1. Make an eventSign in with bWalletX. Set the tiers, how many, the sale window and any age limit.
  2. Paid as you sellMoney from each ticket goes straight to your wallet as it sells.
  3. A door scanner on any phoneOpen the scanner on a phone and check people in. It keeps working if the signal drops.
  4. A room for the eventEvery event gets a room in bChatX for the crowd and the crew.
  5. Splits everyone signedAgree the venue and artist splits in bSignX before tickets go on sale.

Where it is

  • FirstNo-cost RSVP tickets, starting with our own events
  • ComingPaid tickets in BSV, straight to the organiser’s wallet
  • ComingPassing a ticket on at face value
