Add your app to bWallet

Built something on BSV? Put it on the Apps screen of bWallet and bWalletX, one tap away for everyone who opens the wallet. It's free, and a person reviews every app.

Submit your appDeveloper docs

Why add it

One tap from the wallet

Your icon sits in Apps beside the other BSV apps. People open it without typing an address.

Sign-in is already there

Apps open in the wallet's own browser with the wallet connected (BRC-100). Ask for the user's identity key and you have a login, with no passwords to store.

Payments in BSV

Ask the wallet to pay and the user sees one approval sheet with the amount. No card forms, no accounts to set up.

Free

No listing fee and no cut of your sales. We check the app is safe, then add it.

What you need

  • Your app's address, served over https://
  • A name (up to 40 characters) and a category
  • An email address, so we can tell you when it's live (never shown)
  • An icon: we take it from your site. An apple-touch-icon gives the sharpest tile.
  • Optional: a one-line description and a BSV address

What happens after you submit

  1. Your submission is saved for review. Nothing is listed straight away.
  2. A person opens your app and checks it against the list below.
  3. If it passes, we add it with your site's name, tagline and icon, and it ships in the next wallet release.
  4. We email you when it's in bWallet.

Want it on your own phone today? In the wallet, open Apps and tap + Add app. That adds any website to your own Apps screen straight away, saved to your wallet. It doesn't list it for anyone else.

What we check

It works and it's yours

The site loads over https, does what it says, and you made it or run it.

It's safe

No scams, no phishing, nothing asking for seed phrases or private keys, nothing illegal. Payments go through the wallet's approval screen, so users always see what they're paying.

Icon and name

We use your site's own icon and title. Add an apple-touch-icon for the best tile.
