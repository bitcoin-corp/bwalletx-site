History. Where your money went.

Every transaction your account has made, read from the chain. Pick a date range, see what came in and went out, and export it in a form your accountant can use.

Get bWalletX

What it does today

  • LiveFull on-chain history. Every transaction that touched the account’s addresses, not just ones made on this device.
  • LiveDate ranges. 7 days, 30 days, this year, all time, or your own dates.
  • LiveSummary. In, out, fees and net for the range.
  • LiveLabels. Tips, likes, pot payments and agent spend are named where the wallet can tell.
  • LiveCSV export. A spreadsheet of the range, one row per transaction.
  • LivePDF statement. A printable statement of the range. On a phone, open it and use Print › Save as PDF.
  • LiveTokens and NFTs. Buys, sells, listings, cancels, transfers and mints, each showing the token or NFT and the amount. Since 5.1.82.
  • LiveGames, subscriptions and apps. Payments to games, pots and subscriptions, and apps you connected, each with its own label. Since 5.1.82.
  • LiveCategory filters. All, Payments, Tokens, NFTs, Games, Subscriptions, Apps, Social. Exports include the category, asset and app. Since 5.1.82.
  • LiveConnections log. Which apps used the wallet, when, what you allowed them, what each spent, and a button to revoke access. Recorded on each device from 5.1.82.

Open it from the wallet: tap History on the account card. Payments from a pot show there too; see Subscriptions and Pots.

Coming

  • ComingGains and losses. A Gains tab with realised gains and losses per tax year: UK HMRC share-pooling rules in pounds by default, FIFO in dollars elsewhere, BSV tracked as an asset, and a disposals CSV for your accountant. Built and in testing; due in the next release.
