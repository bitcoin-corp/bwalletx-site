The b family

Every service looks like bWalletX: black, warm white, the gold b and a gold button, the house look bChatX and bwalletx.com already share. On its pages each service keeps the gold b, and only the X in its name takes its colour. Its icon is the b in its own colour, so you can tell the services apart in a browser tab, on a phone and on social profiles.

The brandRead the brand guide
One lookBlack ground, warm white text, gold b, gold main button. The same on every service.
One accentEach service owns one colour, and it appears only on its X and a few small details. Never as a background.
Page and iconOn its pages every service keeps the gold b; only the X takes its colour. The coloured b is its icon: favicon, app icon and profile picture. Decided 23 Oct 2026.
Two exceptionsb0-x’s x is white: it hosts all the others. bMarketX’s X is rainbow: it sells everything.

Not in the family: bForgeX (orange and black), bOpenX and bGravityX are client companies. Clients keep their own brands; we never put our b on theirs.
