On b · Using Twetch with bWalletX

Twetch on b

Keep using Twetch, and use bWalletX next to it. The Twetch you know is in the bWalletX Feed, your old Twetch wallet can come with you, and b is on Twetch too.

What you can do

Read Twetch in the Feed

Open Feed and pick the Twetch chip. You see recent public Twetch posts, read from Twetch’s own public API. Tap a name to see that person’s posts and profile.

Tip, like, lock and reply

Tip the author in BSV: it goes to the address of the key their Twetch posts are signed with. Lock BSV behind a post you rate; it stays yours and unlocks on the date shown. Likes and replies you make here are written on chain as bChat posts.

Bookmarks and paid posts stay on Twetch

Twetch bookmarks and paid unlocks live on Twetch’s servers, so those buttons open the post on Twetch. Twetch is also on the Apps tab’s home screen.

Bring your Twetch wallet

Add account › Restore › Twetch, then enter your Twetch 12 words. bWalletX uses the address path Twetch wallets used, so your coins show up. Your words stay on your device.

Pay someone on Twetch

In Send, choose Other wallet › twetch.me to pay name@twetch.me. It is shown in full and marked outside bWalletX, and only offered while Twetch’s paymail answers.

Want to ask b something on Twetch? Read b is on Twetch.
