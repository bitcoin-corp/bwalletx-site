# Wallet theme products

Every user gets bWallet free. Themes come in three tiers.

1. **Free**: colours, ready-made themes and emoji cards, for your own wallet. Personal only; nothing shareable.
2. **Premium themes** (about $1, sold in the app): animated, limited-edition and creator themes you own. The money goes to the creator. Creators are artists and token issuers (memecoins included, also coins launched on our launchpad).
3. **Editions** ($99 a year, made by us): for businesses and token communities. A shareable branded wallet for every holder with its own address (wallet.yourcoin.com), name, card art, icon and the token shown first, featured in Editions and the token's room. We design, check and run it for the year.

**Holders**: anyone holding a token unlocks that token's community theme free, automatically (same as token rooms). Issuers may also sell limited or premium themes.

Status (keep the pages honest): free themes and theme packs are live; premium themes and automatic holder themes are coming; Editions are ordered by message today.
