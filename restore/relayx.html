Coming from RelayX? Everything is still on chain.

RelayX has closed, but your coins, ordinals and NFTs are still on chain at the keys in your RelayX words. bWalletX opens them as an account, with the same keys.

Get bWalletX

What you need

Your RelayX 12 words.

Steps in bWalletX

  1. Install bWalletX and open it.
  2. On the welcome screen choose Restore, then RelayX. (Already have bWalletX? Settings › Add account › RelayX.)
  3. Type your 12 words and set a password. Your BSV and ordinals show straight away.
  4. For your RelayX NFTs, tap the blue Your RelayX collection card at the top of the account. They then show in the NFTs tab under Run.
  5. If it finds BSV on older RelayX addresses, tap Move it into this account.

Just want to look first? Recover RelayX collections shows your NFTs from your words without adding an account. Nothing moves.

What comes across

  • BSV and 1Sat ordinals on your RelayX payment and ordinals keys.
  • Your RelayX NFTs (Run tokens), kept exactly as they are.
  • BSV on older RelayX addresses, moved in with one tap.

Fees

Restoring is free: the keys are the same, so nothing moves. Moving coins from older addresses pays the BSV network fee, a few sats. bWalletX doesn’t charge for restoring or sweeping.

Stay safe

  • Only type your words inside the bWalletX app. Never into a website, a chat, a form, or for anyone offering to “recover” your wallet for you.
  • bWalletX works out every key on your device. Your words are not sent anywhere.
  • bWalletX is in beta: try a small amount first. The sweep screen offers a test sweep before the rest.

Ready?

Get bWalletX on Android, iPhone, Chrome or the web, and bring your RelayX wallet home.
