Coming from Yours Wallet? You already know the way.

bWalletX grew from Yours Wallet and uses the same keys. Your 12 words open the same account, with your coins, ordinals, tokens and names all there.

Get bWalletX

What you need

Your Yours 12 words. You don’t need the Yours extension installed.

Steps in bWalletX

  1. Install bWalletX and open it.
  2. On the welcome screen choose Restore, then the first option, bWalletX. That one covers Yours too. (Already have bWalletX? Settings › Add account › bWalletX.)
  3. Type your 12 words and set a password.
  4. Your account opens as it was in Yours.

What comes across

  • Everything: BSV, 1Sat ordinals, BSV-20 and BSV-21 tokens, and names.
  • Coins on Yours’ older payment key are moved across for you.

Fees

Restoring is free: the keys are the same, so nothing moves. Moving coins off the older payment key pays the BSV network fee, a few sats. bWalletX doesn’t charge for restoring or sweeping.

Stay safe

  • Only type your words inside the bWalletX app. Never into a website, a chat, a form, or for anyone offering to “recover” your wallet for you.
  • bWalletX works out every key on your device. Your words are not sent anywhere.
  • bWalletX is in beta: try a small amount first. The sweep screen offers a test sweep before the rest.

Ready?

Get bWalletX on Android, iPhone, Chrome or the web, and bring your Yours wallet home.
