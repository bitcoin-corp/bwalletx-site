Releases. What’s new.

Every bWalletX release and what it added, newest first.

Each release ships to the Android APK, the Chrome extension and the web wallet. Android also goes to Google Play internal testing. The App Store build is not yet submitted.

5.1.99 2026-10-11

  • A cleaner card front: the adjust icon has moved off the front of your card
  • Design your card: tap the sliders at the top right of the card back to open a full-screen designer
  • See your card large as you change it: drag to move the art, set brightness and position, Reset or Done

5.1.98 2026-10-11

  • Calmer card art: bright bAvatar art is dimmed and moved off to one side, so your balance and buttons stay clear
  • Adjust your card: the sliders icon on the card front sets brightness and moves the art, with Reset if you change your mind
  • The address on the front of your card no longer goes blank; the wallet keeps trying until it loads

5.1.97 2026-10-11

  • Your wallet card now shows your gold bAvatar art on the front, under a light shade so your balance stays easy to read

5.1.96 2026-10-11

  • New approval cards: signing in, connecting, paying and minting each have their own big, clear card, so you can see what you are approving at a glance
  • Payments ask in dollars, with the sats underneath and one big Pay button
  • Anything risky, like a site asking for full control of your keys, shows a red Careful card where the safe choice is the big button
  • The wallet no longer asks you to register a handle you already have
  • Closing the handle screen after you unlock takes you back to your wallet, not Settings

5.1.95 2026-10-11

  • Mint your bAvatar: Mint my bAvatar in the account menu inscribes your gold bAvatar with your name and account number. The first 1,000 accounts mint free
  • Every account without its own picture gets a gold bAvatar drawn from its name, and the back of the wallet card shows it as a QR code any phone camera can scan to reach your paymail
  • Sign in with bWalletX on bChatX asks one clear question, “Prove it’s you”, already ticked, so sign-in no longer fails with “permission denied”
  • Approval sheets on websites always become tappable, and if one ever sticks, Open in bWalletX moves the request into the wallet
  • Money sent to your receive address is ready to spend straight away, including for minting and for websites you connect to
  • The wallet checks your addresses for new payments regularly in the background

5.1.94 2026-10-11

  • bWallet now has free Spaces: join the bWallet Lounge and other public Spaces to listen, ask to speak, speak and chat, with report and block on every person and message
  • bChatX follows your wallet account: switch accounts in the wallet and bChatX signs in as that account. Settings › bChatX lets you use a different name or not connect at all
  • A cleaner wallet card: your identity key sits in the magnetic strip on the back with a copy icon, and a pen icon on the front flips the card
  • An interrupted sync repair now picks up again by itself the next time the wallet opens

5.1.93 2026-10-10

  • Money sent to any address the wallet showed you now always counts in your balance, even if it arrived while the wallet was closed or on another device
  • A storage badge in Settings shows where this account’s records live, when it last synced, and what went wrong if a sync fails, with Repair Sync one tap away
  • The wallet card shows your $handle with your identity key’s fingerprint; the back of the card shows the full Identity key with Copy, and a Sign button flips it
  • Each account card shows that account’s own handle, never another account’s bChatX name
  • Missing BSV-21 tokens can be found again from Settings › Troubleshooting › Find a missing token

5.1.92 2026-10-10

  • Back bWalletX: set aside a pot and support development with $5, $10, $25 or your own amount a month, paid in BSV at that day’s rate. Stop any time and the rest stays in your pot
  • Fewer approval popups in the Chrome extension: one sheet per site with Allow all, and “Don’t ask again” for sign-in and signing. Payments stay inside your monthly allowance
  • bWallet now looks like bWalletX: the same black and gold top bar, dock and home row, with Portfolio vs market one tap from the price
  • bWallet has its own yellow wallet card, matching its app icon; bWalletX keeps its black card

5.1.91 2026-10-10

  • Pots & Locks: the Lock screen groups your locks into pots (savings, pension, bills and more), with every locking feature kept. Back PNEEs takes you from the card to the amount, the lock and a done screen
  • Subscriptions are priced in dollars and paid in BSV at that day’s rate, each from a pot you choose. See them all in one list, pause or cancel any time
  • Portfolio vs market: tap the price tile to compare your wallet with holding BSV, BTC or dollars over a day, week, month, year or all time
  • Swap other coins into BSV from the card on the home screen
  • Every error has Copy and Ask b, and Add account is one screen: new account, restore from 12 words (bWalletX, Yours, Twetch, RelayX) or import a backup
  • Chrome extension: incoming calls ring even with the side panel closed, calls open in their own window or float picture-in-picture, and approvals come one at a time
