Use bWalletX in your browser.

Nothing to install. Open the web wallet, restore with your 12 words or create a new wallet, and you’re in.

Open the web wallet →

Why use the web wallet

  • No installIt runs in a normal browser tab. No app store, no extension.
  • Your keysYour keys are made and kept in your browser. They aren’t sent to us.
  • Any computerWorks on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.
  • Same walletIt’s the same wallet as the app. Restore with your 12 words to see the same coins and tokens, or create a new wallet.

Good to know

The web wallet is in beta: test with small amounts. For daily use we recommend the app or the browser extension, which keep your wallet on your own device rather than in a browser tab.
