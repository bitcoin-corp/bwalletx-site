On b · Using Yours Wallet with bWalletX

Yours on b

bWalletX grew from the open-source Yours Wallet code, so a Yours wallet fits straight in: same keys, same addresses, coins, tokens and ordinals.

What you can do

Restore with your 12 words

Add account › Restore › Yours, then enter your 12 words. They are all you need; the Yours extension is not required.

Or use a Yours backup file

If you saved a Yours backup (a .json file), upload it instead. Its address paths come with it, so a custom setup restores as it was.

Status

What works in bWalletX today, and what doesn’t yet.
