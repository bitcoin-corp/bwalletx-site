bRadio.
Talk live. Keep it.

Live audio Spaces inside bWalletX. Host a conversation, bring people up on stage, and turn the best ones into podcast episodes.

Join the LoungeGet bWalletX

Host a Space

Start a Space from your wallet and talk. Listeners raise a hand; you bring them up on stage when you’re ready. Your followers get a quiet “is hosting” notification, and a gold ring on your avatar shows you’re in a Space.

A live bar and a mini player keep the Space with you while you move around the wallet.

The bRadio Lounge

An open stage that’s always there. Drop in, listen, raise a hand. Join the Lounge.

Keep the good ones

If you choose to record (it’s off unless you turn it on), your Space becomes a podcast episode with a title and credits for your guests. Episodes appear in the @bradio feed on bChatX and in the wallet’s bRadio feed.

Anyone can listen to a free preview: the first minute, unless you set a different window.

Own an episode

An episode can be owned as a Podcast NFT. Owners listen in full for free, and can sell it on the Exchange.

Paid straight to the host

Every payment is a real, tiny payment that goes straight to the host. No middle account, no waiting for a payout.

Spaces are free in every edition. Paid listening is in bWalletX: the direct download, web and desktop.

What’s live

  • LiveSpaces. Host, raise a hand, come up on stage.
  • LiveThe Lounge. An open stage, always on.
  • LivePodcast episodes. Record by choice, with titles, guest credits and a free preview.
  • LivePodcast NFTs. Own an episode, listen in full, sell it on the Exchange.
