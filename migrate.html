Move to bWalletX

Bring everything home.

Coins, NFTs, tokens, names and your posts. bWalletX sweeps them in from the wallet you used before, right on your own device. Moving is free; you only pay the normal network fee, usually a cent or less.

Yours / Panda Wallet

Live
What moves
BSV, 1Sat NFTs, BSV-21 tokens, from the payment, ordinals and identity keys (and the older Yours payment key).
You need
Your 12-word Yours recovery phrase.
  1. In bWalletX, open Settings › Account data › Sweep into this account.
  2. Paste your Yours phrase. All the Yours keys are tried for you.
  3. Check what it found, tick it, tap Sweep.

New to bWalletX? Restoring with your Yours phrase opens the same wallet directly, since bWalletX uses the same keys.

Live

Live

Tokens, ordinals and names

They ride along with any live wallet route above. Here’s how each kind is treated.

1Sat ordinals (NFTs)

Live

Swept as NFTs once their origin is confirmed as a real inscription. Images, music, collectibles and bAvatars all count.

BSV-21 tokens

Live

Found by balance and swept one token at a time, so each lands intact in your bWalletX tokens list.

BSV-20 and unreadable items

Listed

Anything that might be a token but can’t be confirmed is shown with a warning and never swept, because spending it as a plain NFT could burn it.

OpNS names

Live

OpNS names are 1Sat ordinals, so they move with your NFTs. After moving, set one as your name in bWalletX.

Stay safe while you move

  • Never share your phrase or key. Not with b, not with support, not in any chat or form. We will never ask for it.
  • Type it only into bWalletX, on your own device. Only public addresses are looked up online to find your coins.
  • Check the address bar. bWalletX on the web is only ever web.bwalletx.com.
  • Test with a little first. For larger amounts, bWalletX offers a small test sweep before the rest.
  • Afterwards, treat the old phrase as retired. Your coins are now protected by your bWalletX backup, so make that backup now.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No fee from us. Each sweep pays the normal Bitcoin SV network fee, a fraction of a cent for most moves.

It found nothing.

Check every word and add the passphrase if you used one. Then try Custom path. You can also ask b in the app, without ever sharing your phrase.

Is it in the App Store and Play versions?

Yes. Sweep is in every bWalletX and bWallet build, and on web.bwalletx.com.

My wallet isn’t listed.

If it gave you 12 or 24 words, try the Any other BIP39 phrase route. Otherwise email support@bwalletx.com with the wallet’s name (never the phrase) and we’ll look at adding it.
