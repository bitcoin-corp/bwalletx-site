Other ways to reach us

  • Report to b, in the app

    Live

    Tap Report to b on any error screen in bWalletX. It files a report that b reads, and you get a reference number.

  • Email

    Live

    Write to support@bwalletx.com.

  • Report a bug

    Live

    Use the short form on Known issues, where you can also see what we’ve fixed.

  • Security issues: b-Aware

    Live

    Anything touching funds, keys or approvals goes to b-Aware. It stays private and is never posted publicly.

  • The Lounge chat room

    Paused

    bChatX is temporarily unavailable. For now, the Lounge is open at bit-sign.online.

What happens next

  • b reads your report and passes it to the team.
  • The team sorts it and decides what to do.
  • Fixed bugs appear on Known issues. Suggestions appear on Ideas.
  • Security reports are never published before the fix ships.
  • We never publish your email or personal details. A review shows only your first name, and only if you tick the box.
