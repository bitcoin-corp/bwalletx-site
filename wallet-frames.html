Your private things. Opened only by your wallet.

A Wallet Frame is a small window inside our products’ pages that belongs to your wallet, not to the site. It opens your private posts, music, photos and records on your own device. The site around it never sees them.

Coming November 2026. This page says what works today and what doesn’t yet. See the status list below.

Like the card box at a shop

When you pay by card in a shop, you tap a small box that belongs to the payment company, not the shop. The shop gets paid. It never sees your card details. A Wallet Frame does the same for your posts, music, photos and records: the product shows the page, your wallet shows what’s private.

Two names for each product

Every product has a public side and a private side.

What you choose to make public lives at the .com, for anyone to see. What you keep private stays in the frame, for you and the people you share it with.

Why we’re doing this

  • Your keys never leave your wallet. No product site holds them, ours included.
  • Your private things stay private. They’re locked before they leave your device and only opened inside your wallet.
  • You choose what goes public. Nothing moves to the .com unless you say so.
  • A hacked site gives nothing away. It only ever held locked content it couldn’t read.

Questions

Is a Wallet Frame live today?

Not yet. Private minting already works in the wallet. The first Wallet Frame, in bChatX, is planned for November.

Can the product’s company see my private things?

No. The frame is drawn by your wallet. The product’s site only passes along content that’s already locked.

Do I need anything new?

Just bWalletX or bWallet. The frame comes with the wallet.
