How it works

  1. Sign in with bWalletX. Your wallet signs a one-time message. Nothing is spent.
  2. Verify your identity with Veriff, our KYC provider. Coming
  3. Submit your work: a test report, a fix, a finding.
  4. b checks it. b looks for duplicates and checks the work against the code.
  5. The team approves. b proposes a payout; a person on the team approves it.
  6. You are paid in BSV to your bWalletX paymail, and the payout is listed in public on /bounties.

Getting paid

To take paid work you sign in with bWalletX and verify your identity with Veriff (our KYC provider). We pay to your bWalletX paymail.

  • No anonymous contractors. Every contractor has a bWalletX sign-in, a legal name, a country, at least one linked account (X, GitHub or Google) and a Veriff check. Veriff also does the sanctions check.
  • Paid only to your own paymail, yourname@bwalletx.com. We never pay to an address pasted into a form or a message.
  • Verify with Veriff: coming. Until it is live you can sign in, fill in your profile and submit work. Payouts start once you are verified.
  • Security reports stay open to anyone by email. A reward for one needs sign-in and Veriff too.

This is paid work, not an investment. Amounts are set per job and only paid after approval.
