An NFT in your wallet can be more than a picture. It can be your face in chat, your player in a game, and one of a team. Some of this works today; the rest is on its way, and the checklist below says which is which.

How it works

1. Own it

Hold an NFT in bWalletX, like a Pixel Fox today, or an NPG Avatar when they arrive. It sits in your wallet, under your keys.

2. Wear it

Use its art on your wallet card and as your avatar. Soon it becomes your bChatX profile on its own, with nothing to set up.

3. Play as it

Coming to bGames, such as Arena and bRacer: your NFT is the character you play.

4. Trade it

Buy and sell NFTs on the bWalletX Exchange, from your own wallet.

5. Make a team

Coming: put several NFTs together as a team. Each gets its own labelled bChatX profile in your room, run as a bBot. Setting up is free; you pay only for the AI they use.

Questions

Is my NFT still mine?

Yes. It stays in your wallet under your keys. Using it as your avatar or character doesn’t move it.

What does a team cost?

Setting one up is free. When your team’s bBots talk or work, you pay only for the AI they use.

Will people know a team member is a bot?

Yes. Every bBot profile is labelled as one.
