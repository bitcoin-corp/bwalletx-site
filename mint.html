What it costs

Usually well under a cent for a photo

You pay the Bitcoin SV network to store your file, so the price depends on its size. A typical photo costs a fraction of a cent. A 1 MB file is about two cents. A full 10 MB video is about 20 cents. bWalletX adds a 1% fee on top of the network fee. Starting a new collection takes one extra transaction, so it roughly doubles the cost of that first mint.

The wallet shows the exact price in dollars before you confirm. Prices here assume BSV at about $20 and the wallet’s standard network fee; they move with the BSV price. The limit is 10 MB per file; the wallet can shrink a big photo for you.

Contracts and new kinds of instruments

A contract in bWalletX is an agreement that both sides sign. Once it is locked on chain, it pays out by its own rules: money goes where the contract says, when the contract says. b, the wallet’s AI assistant, helps you draft it. It asks who it’s between, what each side agrees to, the price and the dates.

Please read this

Anything that works like an investment, such as shares, bonds or a pension, may be regulated where you live. You are responsible for following the law. bWalletX is a wallet: it does not offer investments, and it can’t tell you whether something you make is allowed. If in doubt, ask a lawyer before you mint or sell it.

Questions

Is it permanent?

Yes. Inscriptions on Bitcoin SV can’t be deleted. So don’t mint anything you don’t own, and nothing illegal.

Who owns it?

Whoever holds it in their wallet. When you mint, that’s you. Your keys stay on your device; we can’t move your things.

Can I sell it?

Yes. List it on the Exchange tab, set a price in dollars, and buyers pay in BSV. You can also just send it to someone.

Does it work on my phone and in Chrome?

Yes. bWalletX runs on Android, iPhone and as a Chrome extension. Contracts, strategies and apps need the full bWalletX (with b, the assistant).
