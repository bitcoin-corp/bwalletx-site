02 / 05  Card rails vs bRand

Same chat. Different money.

Card railsbRand
FeesA percentage of each sale, often plus a fixed chargeBitcoin network fee, usually well under 1p
SettlementTypically days to reach your bankOn chain in seconds, in your wallet
Who holds the moneyThe processor, until it pays outYou. Your keys, your wallet
ChargebacksCan be reversed weeks laterFinal once paid; refunds are yours to give
ReceiptA private record at the processorA public transaction anyone can check
Agent spendingUsually a card or API key with wide accessA capped account with a daily limit and Stop

Card fees vary by provider and country. Sat prices move with the BSV price; the sheet shows the amount before you allow it.

03 / 05  Kept on a short leash

You stay in charge of the money.

  • 01The agent has its own capped account with a daily limit you set. It never touches your main wallet.
  • 02A Stop button pauses it at once.
  • 03Every sale gets a receipt on chain, so you and your customer have the same record.
  • 04Refunds come from your own wallet, by you.
  • 05Customers approve every payment themselves. The agent can ask; it can’t take.
