Talking to b means talking to us.

b is the bWalletX team’s assistant. When you talk to b, you are talking to our company, not writing in a private notebook.

Not private. We read and keep what you tell b, so we can fix bugs and make the app better.

Not end-to-end encrypted. Messages travel over HTTPS like any website, and our server and our AI provider can read them.

Never type your 12 words, private keys or passwords to b. Nobody at bWalletX will ever ask for them.

Who b is

b is an AI model, Claude from Anthropic, run by our server at The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. It answers on our behalf. b is us.

Each message you send b goes to our server, and from there to our AI provider (Anthropic) so it can write b’s reply.

Why we read what you tell b

We are a small team, and we want fast, continuous, honest feedback. When something breaks or confuses you, telling b is the quickest way for it to reach us. We read those conversations and fix things quickly.

It is how a small team listens to everyone at once. That only works if you know we are listening, so we are saying it plainly.

What we keep

  • Your conversations with b, kept on our server for up to 180 days.
  • Only our team and our server can read them. We never sell conversations, and we don’t use them for advertising.
  • Improving b. We may use conversations, with personal details removed, to improve b. You can opt out: email support@bwalletx.com with the subject ‘b opt-out’ and we’ll exclude your chats.
  • Secrets are stripped. The wallet removes anything that looks like a recovery phrase or private key before a message leaves your device, and our server strips secrets again before anything is kept. Don’t rely on that: never type them.
  • Want them deleted? Email support@bwalletx.com and we will delete your conversations with b.

The full detail is in our privacy policy.

What is private in bWalletX, and what isn’t

Private

  • Your keys and your 12 words stay on your device. We never hold them and can’t move your money.
  • Sealed contracts on bChatX are encrypted to the people who sign them.
  • A passkey backup of your 12 words is encrypted so only your passkey opens it.

Not private

  • Chats with b. We read and keep them.
  • Bug reports and ideas you send to b or on /bugs.
  • Rooms and the public feed. Room messages are stored on our server, and feed posts go on the public blockchain.
  • Direct messages are stored on our server. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Questions

Is my chat with b private?

No. b is our team. We read what you tell b and keep it, so we can fix things.

Is it encrypted?

In transit, yes, with HTTPS like any website. It is not end-to-end encrypted, and we can read it. Some saved chat lists on bChatX are locked with your wallet so other people can’t browse them, but that does not make the conversation private from us: each message reaches our server and our AI provider readable, and we keep a readable copy.

Who can read it?

Our team and our server at The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. Each message is also sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write b’s reply.

How long is it kept?

Up to 180 days. Email support@bwalletx.com to ask us to delete it sooner.

Do you use my chats to train AI?

We may use conversations, with personal details removed, to improve b. You can opt out: email support@bwalletx.com with the subject ‘b opt-out’ and we’ll exclude your chats. We never sell conversations.

What should I never tell b?

Your 12 words (recovery phrase), private keys, passwords, backup files or API keys. The wallet tries to catch these and won’t send them, but please don’t type them at all. If you ever did, move your money to a new wallet.

What if I use my own AI key?

Then b’s replies come straight from the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter), and your messages go to that provider, which handles them under its own terms. Anything you report to b still comes to us.

How do I talk privately to a person?

bWalletX doesn’t have an end-to-end encrypted chat today. To reach a person on our team, email support@bwalletx.com; that is ordinary email, read by us. To report a security problem, follow b-Aware.
