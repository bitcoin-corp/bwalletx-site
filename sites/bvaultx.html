What it is

bVaultX: lock BSV for as long as you want your files kept, and the lock pays for storage month by month. Prepaid, time-locked, and yours: you keep the keys and can always get unspent BSV back from your 12 words.

Status: 5 live, 4 coming

  • ✓Lock BSV. Time locks in bWalletX: all at once, or spread daily, weekly or monthly, up to about ten years. bWalletX › Lock
  • ✓Claim when it opens. Matured locks come back to your wallet in one tap. bWalletX › Lock
  • ✓Recover from your 12 words. Locks use your own keys, so a restored wallet still sees and claims them. No server needed.
  • ✓Lock receipts you can check. An optional receipt in the same transaction, checked against the chain. bwalletx.com/lock/verify
  • ✓Pots. Group your locks by what they're for, like a savings goal.
  • ◻Prepaid storage vaults: lock BSV for your files on bDriveX, paid by monthly payments your wallet signs in advance, each time-locked to its month.
  • ◻Stop any time. Stop, and we stop storing; payments for months that haven't come yet stay yours.
  • ◻Keep a Space recording when everyone on stage agrees, stored at the host's cost.
  • ◻Lock other coins in a vault: BTC and ETH, with the same rule that you hold the keys.
