What it is

bRadioX: live radio you can join. Listen in the bRadio Lounge on bChatX, raise your hand to speak, and record shows in Bitcoin Radio. Paid shows and the radio exchange are coming.

Status: 4 live, 3 coming

  • ✓The bRadio Lounge, a public room with live audio on bChatX. Follow it to get a ping when a show goes live.
  • ✓Speak on air. Sign in with bWalletX, raise your hand, and the host brings you on stage.
  • ✓Bitcoin Radio studio: record in the browser and save recordings on-chain from your own bWalletX.
  • ◻A schedule of shows on bCalX, each with its own page and calendar invite.
  • ✓Paid shows, if the host wants: a host can charge by the minute, paid from the listener's own wallet straight to the people on stage. The Lounge is free.
  • ◻The $BRADIO token on the Exchange, with the Lounge as its room.
  • ◻bRadioX, the radio exchange: a token and a room for each show and artist.
