Restore. Bring your coins home.

Have an old BSV wallet? Its 12 words still work. bWalletX can open it as an account, or move what’s in it into one of yours.

Get bWalletX

Restore or sweep?

Restore opens the old wallet as an account in bWalletX. The keys are the same, so nothing moves on chain. Use it when bWalletX knows the wallet’s layout.

Sweep moves the coins and tokens out of the old wallet into an account you already have, in new transactions. Use it for wallets that spread coins across many addresses, or when you just want everything in one place. Sweeping pays a small network fee.

RelayX wallets

RelayX has closed, but everything your RelayX words hold is still on chain. bWalletX brings it back in two ways:

  • Restore it as an account. On the welcome screen (or Add account), choose Restore, pick RelayX and enter your 12 words. Your BSV and 1Sat ordinals come across straight away.
  • Your RelayX NFTs. RelayX NFTs are Run tokens, which spread across many addresses. On the restored account a blue Your RelayX collection card appears at the top: tap it once and bWalletX finds them all. They then show in the NFTs tab under Run, kept exactly as they are.
  • BSV on older RelayX addresses. If the search finds coins outside the account’s main address, tap Move it into this account.
  • Just want to look first? Recover RelayX collections finds and shows your NFTs from your words without adding an account. Nothing moves.

What stays behind, and why

  • Names, BSV-20 tokens and locked coins are not swept. The sweep screen tells you how many it found. Restore the wallet as an account instead to keep them.
  • Money Button tokens (SFP). Each one can only be spent with a signature from Money Button’s token server, which shut down with Money Button in 2022. Nobody can move or burn them alone. bWalletX keeps them out of the BSV sweep so they are not destroyed by accident, and lists them for you.
  • HandCash, Centbee, ElectrumSV and others aren’t listed by name. If you have their 12 or 24 words, try a sweep: bWalletX tries every path above. If it finds nothing, tell us.

How to restore

  1. On the welcome screen, choose to restore a wallet.
  2. Pick the wallet: bWalletX (also Yours and Panda), RelayX, Twetch, SimplyCash (phone app), Restore with private key, Restore from master backup, or Other.
  3. Type your words (or key) and set a password. For Other, turn on Use custom derivations to enter your own paths.

How to sweep

  1. Open Settings › Account data › Sweep into this account.
  2. Paste the recovery phrase, private key (WIF) or xprv. Add the passphrase only if the old wallet had one, and a custom path if you know it.
  3. bWalletX scans every known layout and shows what it found. Check it, then sweep. You get a link to each transaction.

Stay safe

  • Never type your words into a website, a chat or a form. Only into the wallet app itself.
  • bWalletX derives every key on your device. Your words are never sent anywhere and are not stored after a sweep.
  • A sweep moves funds in new transactions. Once they’re in, the old words no longer hold anything you swept.
