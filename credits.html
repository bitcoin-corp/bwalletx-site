Credits. Prepaid, per service.

Credits are prepaid credit that a service issues for its own service. Any service can issue its own credits and choose what it accepts: BSV, MNEE or PNEEs. bWalletX holds them and spends them for you.

Credits are in testing. bCredits for b come first. Nobody can buy or spend credits yet.

bCredits, for b

The first issuer is The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. Its credits, bCredits, pay for b, the agent in bWalletX. These terms are decided:

  • You buy bCredits with BSV. 1 bCredit = 1p.
  • b charges its running cost × 1.35 for each reply.
  • Top up as little as you like, up to £20 at a time.
  • bCredits don’t expire.
  • They’re non-refundable, except where the law says otherwise.
  • They can’t be transferred or cashed out. They’re only for b.
  • Your unspent BSV stays in your own wallet. Only what you top up sits with the issuer.

For services

Any service can issue its own credits. For example, a compute service like Mandala could issue credits for its compute.

Users top up

Charge per use

Debit per request, per minute or per job. No card processor. No monthly billing.

Want to issue credits for your service? Email support@bwalletx.com.

In the wallet

  • Each balance has its own card.
  • The card being spent comes to the front and ticks down as you use it, with a chip like b · 2 bCredits/reply.
  • Tap a card to flip it. The back shows the contract: who issued it, what it’s for, the rate and what you’ve spent so far.
  • Stop ends payment and ends the service.
  • Contact the issuer is on the back of every card.
